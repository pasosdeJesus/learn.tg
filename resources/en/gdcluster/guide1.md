# Guide 1: What is Global Disciples?

**Objective:** Understand that Global Disciples (GD) is an **academy
trainer** and how your church cluster can become an academy that trains
self-sustaining church planters.

---

## What is Global Disciples?

Global Disciples is a Christian organization that for more than **25 years**
has helped leaders in more than **65 countries** create their own academies
for training church planters.

> **Vision:** "That every person have the opportunity to choose and follow
> Jesus Christ."
>
> **Mission:** "To make it possible for church networks to multiply
> Christ-like disciples and locally sustainable congregations."

---

## GD trains the trainers

| Level | Who | What they do |
|-------|-----|--------------|
| **GD** | Global Disciples | Trains academy directors |
| **Academy** | Your church cluster | Trains church planters |
| **Planters** | The graduates | Run their own business, make disciples, and plant churches |

Your cluster of **3 or more churches** becomes an **academy** with a
**director**, **annual courses**, **self-funding**, and **graduates** who go
into the field.

---

## The 3 phases of the academy

| Phase | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **GDT** (Global Disciples Training) | Train church planters (10 days of training + 20 days of practice) |
| **SBD** (Small Business Development) | Teach planters to sustain themselves with a business |
| **LEAD** (Leadership Development) | Form leaders with character, knowledge, and skills |

---

## Financial goals

GD helps you **consolidate your academy** by covering a decreasing share of
the costs. Your cluster covers the rest:

| Year | Your academy covers | GD covers |
|------|---------------------|-----------|
| **1** | 55% | **45%** |
| **2** | 70% | **30%** |
| **3** | 85% | **15%** |
| **4** | **100%** | 0% |

This money funds the **director's salary**, the **materials**, and the
**operating costs**. GD helps you start, but in the end the academy is
**100% your church network**.

> The same scale (45% → 30% → 15% → 0%) applies when you start the SBD phase.

---

## How does your cluster start?

1. **Form your cluster (Guide 2):** invite 3 pastors. Each one registers on
   learn.tg, receives a **44 SLEARN** bonus, takes this course (payable with
   SLEARN and USDT), and creates the cluster.
2. **Save funds (Guide 3):** each completed guide gives scholarships (USDT +
   SLEARN), and **10%** goes to the cluster fund (or to the Sierra Leone fund
   if there is no cluster). You save to invite the trainer and cover your
   share of the first year (55%).
3. **Contact GD (Guide 4):**
   - **Country without GD** (e.g. Sierra Leone): pdJ contacts GD so they assign
     a director from another country; your cluster funds their travel and
     training.
   - **Country with GD** (e.g. Colombia): pdJ connects you with the local
     director.
