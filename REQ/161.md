## 1. Objective

A premium bilingual course that guides pastors through the Global Disciples (GD) cluster
system, plus the platform mechanics it uses: the cluster fund, the presentation of the cluster
to GD, the pastors' approval, the release of the funds and the public ranking. The cluster
model itself is https://github.com/pasosdeJesus/learn.tg/issues/220.

## 3. Cluster wallet

`church.cluster_wallet` of the initiator church, set by the initiator from the cluster page.

- **Validation**: valid EVM address, EIP-55 checksum when mixed case, not the zero address, not a
  platform or `ClusterFundsV2` address; the response reports `wallet_checksummed`.
- **Control**: an account must be a wallet registered in the initiator's account
  (`billetera_usuario`); a multisig must have **one owner per pastor of the cluster**, each owner a
  wallet of that pastor. A contract without `getOwners()` is rejected. Chain read only.
- **Approved by the pastors** in the `wallet` phase (§4.7): the initiator approves first and its
  approval **fixes** the wallet (`wallet_fixed_at`); the other two pastors can only approve after
  that. When the third approves, the state moves to `wallet_approved` and the fund starts existing.
  A change after the initiator's approval is not possible (an extreme case, a compromised wallet,
  is resolved by support, editing the database directly).
- **Before `wallet_approved`** the cluster fund does not exist: the 10 % of purchases and
  scholarships goes to the **country fund**, and donations to the cluster are not accepted.
  **After `wallet_approved`** the fund exists, indexed by that address, and starts growing.
- **Changing it with funds credited** is no longer possible: the wallet is fixed at
  `wallet_approved`. The old `409 cluster_wallet_has_funds` guard is kept only for the
  `forming` state, where the wallet can still be edited.
- `setClusterVerification(wallet, true)` when pdJ moves the presentation to `sent`.

## 4. Presentation, approval and release

### 4.1 States

| State | Who moves it | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `forming` | The initiator | Cluster in formation. The wallet is **not** approved; the cluster fund does not exist; the 10 % goes to the country fund; donations to the cluster are not accepted. |
| `wallet_approved` | The 3 pastors (initiator first) | The wallet is fixed and the fund starts growing. The budget field is unlocked. |
| `waiting_for_budget` | The initiator | Waiting for the fourth-year budget (PDF + total). |
| `waiting_for_pd_job_offer` | The initiator | Budget received; waiting for the director's offer (PDF + pay + hours). |
| `requested` (= `data_approved`) | The 3 pastors | When the third pastor approves the documents, the cluster is **requesting the presentation to GD**. Wallet and data fixed. |
| `under_review` | pdJ | pdJ is reviewing. |
| `returned` | pdJ | Data unlocked; the evaluation is shown. A change invalidates the data approvals (back to `waiting_for_budget` or `waiting_for_pd_job_offer`). The wallet is **not** unlocked. |
| `sent` | pdJ | Sent to GD; the cluster is verified on-chain. |
| `answered` | pdJ | GD answered; the funds are released (§4.4). |
| `cancelled` | The initiator | Withdrawal before `requested`. |

`approved` disappears: `data_approved` and `requested` are the same state. pdJ goes from
`requested` to `sent` (if the documents are fine) or to `returned` (if they need correction).

The fields of `/{lang}/cluster` unlock as the state advances: the budget field appears in
`wallet_approved`, the director's offer in `waiting_for_budget`, the approval buttons in
`waiting_for_pd_job_offer`.

### 4.2 Readiness to request the presentation

The request **is** the third `data` approval: there is no separate request button (§4.7). That
approval is accepted only when: the cluster is `active` with 3+ churches; every principal pastor
completed the course with `profilescore > 90`; every pastor invited at least one pastor/co-pastor
and one member/leader of their own church; the five data are complete (`fourth_year_budget`,
`budget_draft_path`, `director_offer_path`, `director_first_year_pay`,
`director_first_year_hours`); the wallet is approved; and the cluster fund is not empty
(`409 cluster_fund_empty`, `503 cluster_fund_unreadable`).

### 4.3 Two approvals

`cluster_gd_approval` has a `phase` (`wallet` | `data`). Each phase stores its own SHA-256:

- **`wallet`**: the hash of the wallet. The initiator approves first (and from then on cannot
  change it); the other two pastors approve after. When the third approves, the state moves to
  `wallet_approved` and the fund starts growing.
- **`data`**: the hash of the five data. The three pastors approve after the documents are ready.
  When the third approves, the state moves to `requested`.

A change in the data expires the `data` approvals (back to `waiting_for_budget` or
`waiting_for_pd_job_offer`). A change in the wallet is not possible after `wallet_approved`.

`GET|POST /api/cluster/[id]/approval?phase=wallet|data`.

### 4.4 Release on `answered`

`redistributeCountryFunds` splits the whole country fund equally among the presented/verified
clusters that have not received anything yet, then `releaseClusterFunds` sends the answered
cluster's fund to its wallet (`fund_released_at`, amounts, history). The cluster then leaves the
process: its on-chain verification is cleared, it is excluded from later splits and its churches
cannot join another cluster (`403 cluster_completed`, `lib/cluster-membership.ts`). From
`fund_released_at` the 10 % of those churches goes to the country fund. A chain failure is recorded
(`country_fund_failed`, `fund_release_failed`) and does not undo the answer; the transition is
idempotent.

### 4.5 What is presented to GD

When pdJ sends the presentation (`sent`), the cluster data sent to GD include:

- the cluster (name, country, churches);
- the contacts of the pastors and of the initiator pastor;
- the fourth-year budget (PDF + total);
- the director's offer (PDF + pay + expected hours);
- the cluster wallet;
- the funds raised on the platform (cluster fund and country fund balances).

The app stores no GD address and sends no mail: pdJ copies the suggested message from the
presentation control in `/{lang}/gdcluster` and records GD's answer there.

### 4.6 Ranking

`/{lang}/gdcluster/ranking`: public; every cluster except the disbanded ones. A cluster in
`sent`/`answered` is green and loses the Donate button; `requested`/`under_review`/`returned`
carry the amber "in process with pdJ" badge. The early states (`forming`, `wallet_approved`,
`waiting_for_budget`, `waiting_for_pd_job_offer`) are listed too, with a neutral "preparing"
badge and no Donate button: seeing the cluster early is what encourages the churches to form the
fund. Rows show the public alias and link to `/{lang}/clusters/[slug]`.

### 4.7 Decisions for the state machine (2026-11-16)

Agreed with the operator; §4.1–§4.6 are the target, this is how they are built.

**Schema.** `gd_status` becomes `NOT NULL DEFAULT 'forming'` with the CHECK of §6; existing `NULL`
rows become `forming` and rows in `approved` become `requested` (never `sent`: those clusters were
not verified on chain). `cluster_gd_approval` gains `phase text NOT NULL CHECK (phase IN
('wallet','data'))` and its PK becomes `(clustergd_id, usuario_id, phase)`. The existing rows are
duplicated into both phases for the clusters in `requested…answered`, so nobody has to re-approve
what they already approved and the fund of those clusters keeps its meaning.

**Two hashes.** `wallet` is the SHA-256 of the address as written (no normalization, so a checksum
change is a change); `data` is the hash of the five fields that exists today. Changing one datum
expires only the `data` approvals; changing the wallet before `wallet_approved` expires only the
`wallet` ones.

**The wallet is fixed at `wallet_approved`** (the third `wallet` approval), not when the initiator
approves: before that, a change expires the approvals already given; from then on, the wallet
cannot change and the extreme case (a compromised wallet) is support editing the database.
`409 gd_fields_locked` keeps applying to the presentation fields from `requested`.

**`requested` is the third `data` approval**, not a button: `POST gd-presentation` stays for pdJ's
transitions and for `cancelled`. The readiness checks of §4.2 (`409 cluster_fund_empty`, `503
cluster_fund_unreadable`) run at that approval, so the pastor who approves last sees the reason.
`GET /api/cluster/[id]` returns `approvals { wallet, data }` so the page shows who is missing.

**The fund opens at `wallet_approved`.** `resolveGDClusterDestination`
(`packages/gdcluster/src/lib/gd-cluster-routing.ts`) requires `gd_status` in `wallet_approved`,
`waiting_for_budget`, `waiting_for_pd_job_offer`, `requested`, `under_review`, `returned`, `sent`
**and** `fund_released_at IS NULL`; `forming`, `cancelled`, `answered` and anything after the
release route the 10 % to the **country fund** (decided: `answered` routes to the country even when
a failed release still leaves money in the cluster fund; retrying the release only sends what is
already there). `donation_wallet` in `packages/gdcluster/src/routes/gd-public-clusters.ts` uses the
same condition, so the Donate button does not appear before the fund exists. Nothing is moved
retroactively: what the country fund received before the approval stays there.

**Membership is frozen from `wallet_approved`.** Joining, leaving and accepting invitations answer
`409 cluster_membership_locked` in every state from `wallet_approved` on (including `returned` and
`answered`); only `forming` and `cancelled` allow it. The unanimity of three approvals, and the
`403 cluster_completed` rule after the release, cannot be maintained otherwise.

**History.** One `clustergd_history` event per transition and one per approval, the approval one
carrying its `phase`: that is what makes the "who moved what" of §4.1 auditable.

**The data cards open in order, and the fund comes first.** The budget waits for `wallet_approved`,
the director's offer for a complete budget, and the data approval for a complete offer; the page
says *why* each card is closed instead of showing a disabled form. Funding must happen **before**
the data approval: the third `data` approval is the request and `cluster_fund_empty` refuses it, so
a spec (and a cluster) that funds afterwards never reaches `requested`.

**Removing `approved`** touches `TRANSITIONS` and `LOCKING_STATUSES` in
`packages/gdcluster/src/lib/gd-presentation.ts`, the badge lists of `RankingClient.tsx:56`, the
state selector and table of `/{lang}/admin/clusters`, and the CHECK of the migration.

## 5. Private cluster page (`/{lang}/cluster`)

The only private page (`/{lang}/cluster/[id]` no longer exists). The cards follow the order of the
states of §4.1 — the wallet first, because the fund does not exist until the pastors approve it:

1. name, status (*Forming* / *In Progress* / *Presented*) and the **public alias** (editable by
   the initiator);
2. **Member Churches** — each row with its members in learn.tg, its pastor's referrals and
   `Leave cluster` at the end;
3. **Invitations** — referred pastors and invitation by wallet, with the state of each invitation
   and one re-invite after a rejection;
4. **Cluster wallet** — the address (editable by the initiator only while `forming` and before it
   approves) and, under the subheading **Pastors approval for the cluster wallet**, the
   `wallet` approval of the pastors with `{{approved}} of {{pastors}}`. The initiator approves
   first and that fixes the address (the "change wallet" button disappears); only then do the other
   two see the approve button. The third approval closes the phase;
5. **Cluster fund balance** — "not created yet: the fund exists once the three pastors approve the
   wallet" until `wallet_approved`;
6. **Country fund balance** (`getCountryFunds`), which exists from the start;
7. **Donations to the cluster fund** (`transaction`, `subcategoria = 'cluster'`), with the total and
   the count, and a line saying donations open with the wallet approval;
8. **Fourth year budget** (`clustergd.fourth_year_budget`), editable from `wallet_approved`;
9. **Program Director**, from `waiting_for_budget`;
10. **Pastors approval** (the `data` phase only, with the button "I know and approve the data"),
    from `waiting_for_pd_job_offer`; the wallet approval lives in card 4;
11. **Present to Global Disciples** (state, pdJ's evaluation, GD's answer and cancel);
12. **History**.

Each data card shows **why** it is not open yet instead of a disabled form ("Locked until the three
pastors approve the wallet", "until the fourth-year budget is saved", "opens when the budget and the
director offer are complete"), the header carries the state of the presentation (§4.1), and the
per-pastor conditions of the presentation card are one mark per condition (course, profile, invited
pastor, invited member) with the detail in a tooltip.

A cluster still being formed shows the name, the member churches, the invitations, the wallet
card and the history. The presentation is requested **only** here: the "Your cluster" card on the
course page links to this page instead of repeating the control.

`GET /api/cluster/[id]` returns, besides the cluster, `funds { cluster, country }`,
`donations { count, usdt_total, slearn_total }`, the state of the two approvals and, per member,
`learners`, `pastor_name` and `pastor_referrals`. A chain read that fails returns `null` and the
card says "Not available".

`director_first_year_pay`, `director_first_year_hours`, `director_fixed_at`, `fund_released_at`,

`cluster_gd_approval` (`clustergd_id`, `usuario_id`, `phase`, `data_hash`, `created_at`; PK on
`(clustergd_id, usuario_id, phase)`) records who approved and against which data.

`cluster/[id]/gd-presentation` (GET, POST, PUT, DELETE),
`cluster/[id]/approval?phase=wallet|data` (GET, POST), `cluster/[id]/budget` (PUT),
`cluster/[id]/director` (PUT), `cluster/[id]/document` (POST, GET, DELETE; PDF ≤ 5 MB in
`uploads/cluster/{id}/`, never in `public/`), `cluster/invitation/wallet`,
`clusters` (GET, public) and `clusters/[slug]` (GET, public), `admin/clusters*` (pdJ).
