## 1. Objective

A premium bilingual course that guides pastors through the Global Disciples (GD) cluster
system, plus the platform mechanics it uses: the cluster fund, the presentation of the cluster
to GD, the pastors' approval, the release of the funds and the public ranking. The cluster
model itself is https://github.com/pasosdeJesus/learn.tg/issues/220.

## 3. Cluster wallet

`church.cluster_wallet` of the initiator church, set by the initiator from the cluster page.

- **Validation**: valid EVM address, EIP-55 checksum when mixed case, not the zero address, not a
  platform or `ClusterFundsV2` address; the response reports `wallet_checksummed`.
- **Control**: an account must be a wallet registered in the initiator's account
  (`billetera_usuario`); a multisig must have **one owner per pastor of the cluster**, each owner a
  wallet of that pastor. A contract without `getOwners()` is rejected. Chain read only.
- **Approved by the pastors** in the `wallet_approved` state (§4): the initiator approves first
  (and loses the ability to change it), then the other two pastors. Once the wallet is approved it
  is **fixed**: it cannot be changed (an extreme case, a compromised wallet, is resolved by
  support, editing the database directly).
- **Before `wallet_approved`** the cluster fund does not exist: the 10 % of purchases and
  scholarships goes to the **country fund**, and donations to the cluster are not accepted.
  **After `wallet_approved`** the fund exists, indexed by that address, and starts growing.
- **Changing it with funds credited** is no longer possible: the wallet is fixed at
  `wallet_approved`. The old `409 cluster_wallet_has_funds` guard is kept only for the
  `forming` state, where the wallet can still be edited.
- `setClusterVerification(wallet, true)` when pdJ moves the presentation to `sent`.

## 4. Presentation, approval and release

### 4.1 States

| State | Who moves it | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `forming` | The initiator | Cluster in formation. The wallet is **not** approved; the cluster fund does not exist; the 10 % goes to the country fund; donations to the cluster are not accepted. |
| `wallet_approved` | The 3 pastors (initiator first) | The wallet is fixed and the fund starts growing. The budget field is unlocked. |
| `waiting_for_budget` | The initiator | Waiting for the fourth-year budget (PDF + total). |
| `waiting_for_pd_job_offer` | The initiator | Budget received; waiting for the director's offer (PDF + pay + hours). |
| `requested` (= `data_approved`) | The 3 pastors | When the third pastor approves the documents, the cluster is **requesting the presentation to GD**. Wallet and data fixed. |
| `under_review` | pdJ | pdJ is reviewing. |
| `returned` | pdJ | Data unlocked; the evaluation is shown. A change invalidates the data approvals (back to `waiting_for_budget` or `waiting_for_pd_job_offer`). The wallet is **not** unlocked. |
| `sent` | pdJ | Sent to GD; the cluster is verified on-chain. |
| `answered` | pdJ | GD answered; the funds are released (§4.4). |
| `cancelled` | The initiator | Withdrawal before `requested`. |

`approved` disappears: `data_approved` and `requested` are the same state. pdJ goes from
`requested` to `sent` (if the documents are fine) or to `returned` (if they need correction).

The fields of `/{lang}/cluster` unlock as the state advances: the budget field appears in
`wallet_approved`, the director's offer in `waiting_for_budget`, the approval buttons in
`waiting_for_pd_job_offer`.

### 4.2 Readiness to request the presentation

The initiator requests the presentation only when: the cluster is `active` with 3+ churches;
every principal pastor completed the course with `profilescore > 90`; every pastor invited at
least one pastor/co-pastor and one member/leader of their own church; the five data are complete
(`fourth_year_budget`, `budget_draft_path`, `director_offer_path`, `director_first_year_pay`,
`director_first_year_hours`); the wallet is approved; and **every pastor approved the data**.

### 4.3 Two approvals

`cluster_gd_approval` has a `phase` (`wallet` | `data`). Each phase stores its own SHA-256:

- **`wallet`**: the hash of the wallet. The initiator approves first (and from then on cannot
  change it); the other two pastors approve after. When the third approves, the state moves to
  `wallet_approved` and the fund starts growing.
- **`data`**: the hash of the five data. The three pastors approve after the documents are ready.
  When the third approves, the state moves to `requested`.

A change in the data expires the `data` approvals (back to `waiting_for_budget` or
`waiting_for_pd_job_offer`). A change in the wallet is not possible after `wallet_approved`.

`GET|POST /api/cluster/[id]/approval?phase=wallet|data`.

### 4.4 Release on `answered`

`redistributeCountryFunds` splits the whole country fund equally among the presented/verified
clusters that have not received anything yet, then `releaseClusterFunds` sends the answered
cluster's fund to its wallet (`fund_released_at`, amounts, history). The cluster then leaves the
process: its on-chain verification is cleared, it is excluded from later splits and its churches
cannot join another cluster (`403 cluster_completed`, `lib/cluster-membership.ts`). From
`fund_released_at` the 10 % of those churches goes to the country fund. A chain failure is recorded
(`country_fund_failed`, `fund_release_failed`) and does not undo the answer; the transition is
idempotent.

### 4.5 What is presented to GD

When pdJ sends the presentation (`sent`), the cluster data sent to GD include:

- the cluster (name, country, churches);
- the contacts of the pastors and of the initiator pastor;
- the fourth-year budget (PDF + total);
- the director's offer (PDF + pay + expected hours);
- the cluster wallet;
- the funds raised on the platform (cluster fund and country fund balances).

The app stores no GD address and sends no mail: pdJ copies the suggested message from the
presentation control in `/{lang}/gdcluster` and records GD's answer there.

### 4.6 Ranking

`/{lang}/gdcluster/ranking`: public; every cluster except the disbanded ones. A cluster in
`sent`/`answered` is green and loses the Donate button; `requested`/`under_review`/`returned`
carry the amber "in process with pdJ" badge. Rows show the public alias and link to
`/{lang}/clusters/[slug]`.

## 5. Private cluster page (`/{lang}/cluster`)

The only private page (`/{lang}/cluster/[id]` no longer exists). Cards, in order:

1. name, status and the **public alias** (editable by the initiator);
2. **Member Churches** — each row with its members in learn.tg, its pastor's referrals and
   `Leave cluster` at the end;
3. **Invitations** — referred pastors and invitation by wallet, with the state of each invitation
   and one re-invite after a rejection;
4. **Cluster fund balance** and **Country fund balance** (`getClusterFunds`, `getCountryFunds`);
   the cluster fund shows "not created yet" until `wallet_approved`;
5. **Donations to the cluster fund** (`transaction`, `subcategoria = 'cluster'`); disabled until
   `wallet_approved`;
6. **Cluster wallet** (with the approval buttons of the pastors);
7. **Fourth year budget** (`clustergd.fourth_year_budget`), unlocked at `wallet_approved`;
8. **Program Director**, unlocked at `waiting_for_budget`;
9. **Pastors approval** (the `data` phase), unlocked at `waiting_for_pd_job_offer`;
10. **Present to Global Disciples** (request form, state, pdJ's evaluation, GD's answer and
    cancel);
11. **History**.

A cluster still being formed shows the name, the member churches, the invitations, the wallet
card and the history. The presentation is requested **only** here: the "Your cluster" card on the
course page links to this page instead of repeating the control.

`GET /api/cluster/[id]` returns, besides the cluster, `funds { cluster, country }`,
`donations { count, usdt_total, slearn_total }`, the state of the two approvals and, per member,
`learners`, `pastor_name` and `pastor_referrals`. A chain read that fails returns `null` and the
card says "Not available".

## 6. Schema and routes

`clustergd`: `gd_status` (CHECK
`forming|wallet_approved|waiting_for_budget|waiting_for_pd_job_offer|requested|under_review|returned|sent|answered|cancelled`),
`gd_requested_at/by`, `gd_reviewed_at`, `gd_returned_at`, `gd_sent_at/by`, `gd_answered_at`,
`gd_evaluation`, `gd_suggestions`, `gd_response/at/by`, `gd_contact_email`,
`fourth_year_budget`, `budget_draft_path`, `budget_fixed_at`, `director_offer_path`,
`director_first_year_pay`, `director_first_year_hours`, `director_fixed_at`, `fund_released_at`,
`fund_released_amount`, `fund_released_slearn`, `status`, `initiator_church_id`, `slug`.
`clustergd_history.event_type` is `varchar(40)`.
`cluster_gd_approval` (`clustergd_id`, `usuario_id`, `phase`, `data_hash`, `created_at`; PK on
`(clustergd_id, usuario_id, phase)`) records who approved and against which data.

Routes (engine, thin wrappers in `app/api`): `cluster/[id]` (GET, PATCH),
`cluster/[id]/gd-presentation` (GET, POST, PUT, DELETE),
`cluster/[id]/approval?phase=wallet|data` (GET, POST), `cluster/[id]/budget` (PUT),
`cluster/[id]/director` (PUT), `cluster/[id]/document` (POST, GET, DELETE; PDF ≤ 5 MB in
`uploads/cluster/{id}/`, never in `public/`), `cluster/invitation/wallet`,
`clusters` (GET, public) and `clusters/[slug]` (GET, public), `admin/clusters*` (pdJ).
