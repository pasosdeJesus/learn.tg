## 1. Objective

A premium bilingual course that guides pastors through the Global Disciples (GD) cluster
system, plus the platform mechanics it uses: the cluster fund, the presentation of the cluster
to GD, the pastors' approval, the release of the funds and the public ranking. The cluster
model itself is https://github.com/pasosdeJesus/learn.tg/issues/220.

## 3. Cluster wallet

`church.cluster_wallet` of the initiator church, set by the initiator from the cluster page.

- **Validation**: valid EVM address, EIP-55 checksum when mixed case, not the zero address, not a
  platform or `ClusterFundsV2` address; the response reports `wallet_checksummed`.
- **Control**: an account must be a wallet registered in the initiator's account
  (`billetera_usuario`); a multisig must have **one owner per pastor of the cluster**, each owner a
  wallet of that pastor. A contract without `getOwners()` is rejected. Chain read only.
- **Locked** while the presentation is `requested`, `under_review`, `approved`, `sent` or
  `answered` (`409 gd_fields_locked`), **for the initiator and for the verifier** (the admin church
  route answers the same code and the modal disables the field); unlocked in `returned` and
  `cancelled`. `answered` is terminal, so after the release the wallet never changes again; an
  extreme case (a compromised wallet between the presentation and the release) is resolved by
  support, editing the database directly.
- **Changing it with funds credited** asks for confirmation (`409 cluster_wallet_has_funds`),
  because the contract fund is indexed by address.
- `setClusterVerification(wallet, true)` when pdJ moves the presentation to `sent`.

## 4. Presentation, approval and release

**Readiness.** The initiator requests the presentation only when: the cluster is `active` with 3+
churches; every principal pastor completed the course with `profilescore > 90`; every pastor
invited at least one pastor/co-pastor and one member/leader of their own church; the five data are
complete (`fourth_year_budget`, `budget_draft_path`, `director_offer_path`,
`director_first_year_pay`, `director_first_year_hours`); the wallet is set; and **every pastor
approved**.

**Approval.** Each principal pastor approves from the cluster page; `cluster_gd_approval` stores
the SHA-256 of the five data plus the wallet, so a change expires the approvals by itself
(`GET|POST /api/cluster/[id]/approval`).

**States.** `requested` (initiator) → `under_review` → `returned` (data and wallet unlocked) →
`approved` → `sent` (the cluster is verified on-chain) → `answered` (funds released). pdJ moves
them with `PUT /api/cluster/[id]/gd-presentation`, carrying the evaluation, the suggestions, GD's
answer and the contact; the initiator is notified on `returned` and `answered`. Every change is
recorded in `clustergd_history` and `userevent`. The app stores no GD address and sends no mail:
pdJ copies the suggested message and records the answer.

**No money, no presentation.** `POST gd-presentation` reads `getClusterFunds(wallet)` and answers
`409 cluster_fund_empty` (with how the fund grows) or `503 cluster_fund_unreadable`; pdJ's `sent`
checks it again so nothing fails silently.

**Release on `answered`.** `redistributeCountryFunds` splits the whole country fund equally among
the presented/verified clusters that have not received anything yet, then `releaseClusterFunds`
sends the answered cluster's fund to its wallet (`fund_released_at`, amounts, history). The
cluster then leaves the process: its on-chain verification is cleared, it is excluded from later
splits and its churches cannot join another cluster (`403 cluster_completed`,
`lib/cluster-membership.ts`). From `fund_released_at` the 10 % of those churches goes to the
country fund. A chain failure is recorded (`country_fund_failed`, `fund_release_failed`) and does
not undo the answer; the transition is idempotent (the `UPDATE` is conditional, so a second answer
gets `409 stale_status`, and a failed release can be retried).

**Ranking** (`/{lang}/gdcluster/ranking`): public; every cluster except the disbanded ones. A
cluster in `sent`/`answered` is green and loses the Donate button; `requested`/`under_review`/
`returned`/`approved` carry the amber "in process with pdJ" badge. Rows show the public alias and
link to `/{lang}/clusters/[slug]`.

## 5. Private cluster page (`/{lang}/cluster`)

The only private page (`/{lang}/cluster/[id]` no longer exists). Cards, in order:

1. name, status (*Formed* / *In Progress*) and the **public alias** (editable by the initiator);
2. **Member Churches** — each row with its members in learn.tg, its pastor's referrals and
   `Leave cluster` at the end;
3. **Invitations** — referred pastors and invitation by wallet, with the state of each invitation
   and one re-invite after a rejection;
4. **Cluster fund balance** and **Country fund balance** (`getClusterFunds`, `getCountryFunds`);
5. **Donations to the cluster fund** (`transaction`, `subcategoria = 'cluster'`);
6. **Cluster wallet**;
7. **Fourth year budget** (`clustergd.fourth_year_budget`: GD funds the first three years, so the
   plan that matters is year 4);
8. **Program Director**;
9. **Pastors approval**;
10. **Present to Global Disciples** (request form, state, pdJ's evaluation and suggestions, GD's
    answer and cancel);
11. **History**.

A cluster still being formed shows the name, the member churches, the invitations and the
history. The presentation is requested **only** here: the "Your cluster" card on the course page
links to this page instead of repeating the control.

`GET /api/cluster/[id]` returns, besides the cluster, `funds { cluster, country }`,
`donations { count, usdt_total, slearn_total }` and, per member, `learners`, `pastor_name` and
`pastor_referrals`. A chain read that fails returns `null` and the card says "Not available".

## 6. Schema and routes

`clustergd`: `gd_status` (CHECK `requested|under_review|returned|approved|sent|answered|cancelled`),
`gd_requested_at/by`, `gd_reviewed_at`, `gd_returned_at`, `gd_approved_at`, `gd_sent_at/by`,
`gd_answered_at`, `gd_evaluation`, `gd_suggestions`, `gd_response/at/by`, `gd_contact_email`,
`fourth_year_budget`, `budget_draft_path`, `budget_fixed_at`, `director_offer_path`,
`director_first_year_pay`, `director_first_year_hours`, `director_fixed_at`, `fund_released_at`,
`fund_released_amount`, `fund_released_slearn`, `status`, `initiator_church_id`, `slug`.
`clustergd_history.event_type` is `varchar(40)`. `cluster_gd_approval` (`clustergd_id`,
`usuario_id`, `data_hash`, `created_at`; PK on the pair) records who approved and against which
data.

Routes (engine, thin wrappers in `app/api`): `cluster/[id]` (GET, PATCH),
`cluster/[id]/gd-presentation` (GET, POST, PUT, DELETE), `cluster/[id]/approval` (GET, POST),
`cluster/[id]/budget` (PUT), `cluster/[id]/director` (PUT), `cluster/[id]/document`
(POST, GET, DELETE; PDF ≤ 5 MB in `uploads/cluster/{id}/`, never in `public/`),
`cluster/invitation/wallet`, `clusters` (GET, public) and `clusters/[slug]` (GET, public),
`admin/clusters*` (pdJ).
