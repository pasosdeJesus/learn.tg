## 1. Objective

Design and implement the pedagogical content for a premium course that guides pastors
through the Global Disciples cluster system, and the platform mechanics the course uses: the
cluster fund, the presentation of the cluster to Global Disciples (GD), the pastors' approval
and the release of the funds. The course uses the existing learn.tg platform; the cluster
model itself is https://github.com/pasosdeJesus/learn.tg/issues/152 and
https://github.com/pasosdeJesus/learn.tg/issues/220.

## 4. Rewards and price

| Reward | Per guide | Formula | Notes |
|--------|-----------|---------|-------|
| **USDT** | 1 USDT | `floor(configuredUsdt × profileScore / 100)` | 6 decimals (1 USDT = 1,000,000); paid from the course vault |
| **SLEARN** | 5 SLEARN | `floor(configuredSlearn × profileScore / 100)` | 2 decimals (1 SLEARN = 100); paid from the course vault |

**Scholarship split (#196):** 80% student, 10% referrer (if referred), 10% cluster fund (or
country fund when the student's church is in no cluster). The backend routes that 10% to
`ClusterFundsV2` and records it as `transaction.type = 'cluster_contribution'`.

**Formula:** smallest units, integer `floor()`. `profileScore = 50` → 0.5 USDT + 2.5 SLEARN;
`profileScore = 100` → 1 USDT + 5 SLEARN.

**Insufficient vault:** that token is not paid, nothing is deferred, no debt is recorded and
the course does not mark the guide as received. The course list shows three indicators per
course (guides completed, scholarships paid in USDT and in SLEARN): yellow means the vault
can pay at least one more guide, gray means it has no funds
(`apps/web/lib/course-progress.ts`). Guide 3 tells the student to wait for yellow; the
behavior is also in the user guide Web3 & UBI — guide 3 and `ARCHITECTURE.md` §Reward System.

**Price** (anchored at Sierra Leone 0.50 USDT and the United States 3.00 USDT, no ceiling):

| Country | HDI | USDT | SLEARN (10% discount, rate 22) |
|---------|-----|------|--------------------------------|
| Sierra Leone | 0.467 | 0.50 | 9.90 |
| Ghana | 0.628 | 1.35 | 26.73 |
| Colombia | 0.788 | 2.20 | 43.56 |
| United States | 0.938 | 3.00 | 59.40 |
| Other | from `m_hdi` | line between the anchors | same conversion |

`packages/rewards/src/lib/premium-pricing.ts` (`calculatePremiumPriceUsdt`,
`calculatePremiumPriceSlearn`) and `premium-purchase` validate the same number server-side.
The SLEARN price is computed at request time from the contract's `usdtToSlearnRate`
(adjustable in [10, 22]) with the 10% discount; a purchase that includes SLEARN is rejected
with 503 when the rate cannot be read, and a purchase without country or HDI with 400.

## 8. Cluster wallet

The wallet lives in `church.cluster_wallet` of the initiator church and the initiator sets it
from the cluster page.

- **Validation** (`validateClusterWallet`): a valid EVM address, EIP-55 checksum when it comes
  in mixed case, not the zero address, and not an address of the platform (backend, referrals,
  churches, treasury, UBI, reserves, verifiers) or of `ClusterFundsV2`. The response reports
  `wallet_checksummed` so the UI can warn about an all-lowercase paste.
- **Control** (`checkWalletControl`): a normal account must be one of the wallets registered in
  the initiator's account (`billetera_usuario`); a multisig must have **one owner per pastor
  of the cluster**, each owner a wallet registered in that pastor's account (one-to-one
  matching, so one pastor cannot hold two seats). A contract without `getOwners()` is
  rejected. It is a chain read, no funds move.
- **Locked** while the presentation is `requested`, `under_review`, `approved`, `sent` or
  `answered` (`409 gd_fields_locked`); **unlocked** in `returned` (pdJ sent it back with
  suggestions) and in `cancelled` (the initiator withdrew the request).
- **Changing it with funds already credited** asks for confirmation (`409
  cluster_wallet_has_funds`) because the contract fund is indexed by address.
- **Verified in the contract** means **presented to GD**: `setClusterVerification(wallet,
  true)` is called when pdJ moves the presentation to `sent`.

## 9. Presentation to Global Disciples

**Readiness.** The initiator can request the presentation only when all of this holds:

1. the cluster is `active` with at least 3 churches;
2. every principal pastor of those churches completed the GD course and has `profilescore >
   90`;
3. every pastor invited at least one pastor or co-pastor and at least one member or leader of
   their own church (`referralrelationship`);
4. the five data are complete: `first_year_budget`, `budget_draft_path`,
   `director_offer_path`, `director_first_year_pay`, `director_first_year_hours`;
5. the cluster wallet is set;
6. **every pastor pressed the approve button**.

**Approval.** Each principal pastor of a member church approves from the cluster page; the
approval is stored in `cluster_gd_approval` with the SHA-256 of the five data **plus the
wallet**, so it covers exactly what will be sent to GD and expires by itself when any of them
changes. `GET|POST /api/cluster/[id]/approval`.

**States.**

| State | Who sets it | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `requested` | The initiator, after every approval | Budget, director and wallet fixed; the request enters pdJ's queue |
| `under_review` | pdJ | pdJ is reviewing |
| `returned` | pdJ | Data and wallet unlocked; evaluation and suggestions shown; a change invalidates the approvals |
| `approved` | pdJ | Data and wallet fixed again |
| `sent` | pdJ | Message sent to GD; the cluster is verified in the contract |
| `answered` | pdJ | GD's answer recorded; the funds are released (§10); the initiator is notified |

> **No money in the fund = validation error.** A cluster is presented only when its fund
> has money: when the initiator asks (`POST gd-presentation`) the backend reads
> `getClusterFunds(wallet)` and answers `409 cluster_fund_empty` with a message that says how
> the fund grows (the 10% of its churches' purchases and scholarships, the donation link); if
> the chain cannot be read it answers `503 cluster_fund_unreadable`. pdJ's `sent` checks it
> again and refuses with the same `409 cluster_fund_empty` instead of a bare `502`
> (`Cluster not found`), so nothing fails silently. The fund is what registers the cluster in
> `ClusterFundsV2`, and that registration is what makes `setClusterVerification` work.

`GET|POST|PUT|DELETE /api/cluster/[id]/gd-presentation`: the initiator requests and cancels;
pdJ moves the state with its evaluation, suggestions, the answer and the channel. Every change
is recorded in `clustergd_history` and `userevent`. pdJ copies the suggested email from the
presentation control in `/{lang}/gdcluster` (the app stores no recipient and sends no mail) and
records GD's answer there; `/{lang}/admin/clusters` is the verifier's cluster administration
(list, status, rename, disband).

## 10. Funds and ranking

**Country fund.** It receives the 10% of GD sales and guide scholarships of churches that are
in no cluster or whose cluster fund no longer exists (because it was released), plus the
donations sent to the country from the ranking.

**Who releases the fund.** The fund is no longer released by a per-pastor **withdrawal** vote:
the old `cluster_fund_vote` table and the "Withdraw" button per pastor were removed. Pastors
still act, but to **approve the presentation** (§9); the fund is released automatically when GD
answers.

**Release when GD answers.** The `answered` transition runs automatically:

1. `redistributeCountryFunds(countryCode, wallets)` with the presented (`sent`/`answered`)
   clusters of the country that exist, are verified on-chain **and have not received their
   funds yet** (`fund_released_at` null): the whole country fund is split **equally among
   them** and credited to each cluster fund;
2. `releaseClusterFunds(wallet)` sends the answered cluster's fund to its wallet and records
   `fund_released_at`, the USDT/SLEARN amounts and the history; the cluster then **leaves the
   process with GD**: its on-chain verification is cleared
   (`setClusterVerification(wallet, false)`) so a later distribution cannot include it again,
   and its churches fall under the no-reentry rule below.

An empty country fund or a cluster that does not exist/is not verified on-chain is skipped. A
chain failure is recorded (`country_fund_failed`, `fund_release_failed`) and does not undo the
registered answer. From `fund_released_at` the 10% of those churches goes to the country fund,
because the cluster fund no longer exists.

**Ranking.** Every cluster is listed except the disbanded ones. A cluster whose presentation
reached `sent` or `answered` is shown in **green** and loses the Donate button; the clusters in
process with pdJ (`requested`/`under_review`/`returned`/`approved`) show an amber "in process
with pdJ" badge. The green depends on the **state**, not on the on-chain flag: the contract is
marked when pdJ moves the presentation to `sent`, and if that call fails the state does not
advance (the route answers `502`) and the cluster stays in process. Clusters do not leave the
ranking.

**Proposal.** https://github.com/pasosdeJesus/learn.tg/issues/294 proposes that the cluster
whose presentation GD answers receives 50% of the country fund instead of the equal split;
it needs a new contract function and is not part of this implementation.

## 11. Schema and routes

`clustergd`: `gd_status` (CHECK `requested|under_review|returned|approved|sent|answered|cancelled`),
`gd_requested_at/by`, `gd_reviewed_at`, `gd_returned_at`, `gd_approved_at`, `gd_sent_at/by`,
`gd_answered_at`, `gd_evaluation`, `gd_suggestions`, `gd_response/at/by`, `gd_contact_email`,
`first_year_budget`, `budget_draft_path`, `budget_fixed_at`, `director_offer_path`,
`director_first_year_pay`, `director_first_year_hours`, `director_fixed_at`,
`fund_released_at`, `fund_released_amount`, `fund_released_slearn`, `pseudonym`, `status`,
`initiator_church_id`. `clustergd_history.event_type` is `varchar(40)`. The retired per-pastor
withdrawal vote table `cluster_fund_vote` and the column `clustergd.ranking_exited_at` were
dropped because the approval (§9) replaced the vote; `cluster_gd_approval` is the table that
records who approved and against which data hash.

`cluster_gd_approval` (`clustergd_id`, `usuario_id`, `data_hash`, `created_at`; PK on the pair).

Routes: `cluster/[id]/gd-presentation` (GET, POST, PUT, DELETE), `cluster/[id]/approval`
(GET, POST), `cluster/[id]/budget` (PUT), `cluster/[id]/director` (PUT),
`cluster/[id]/document` (POST, GET, DELETE; PDF ≤ 5 MB in `uploads/cluster/{id}/`, never in
`public/`), `admin/clusters*` (pdJ). The engine routes are thin wrappers over the core.

Two harness bugs found and fixed while running them (no product change):

1. **The page restores the puzzle instead of fetching it.** `GET /api/crossword` is only

   from `localStorage`. `openCrosswordOnline` only accepted the network response, so guide 1 was
   reported as "no crossword" for every pastor even with the board rendered (`celdas 29-32`,

   `/api/check-crossword` response arrives; that was counted as a failure. It now counts as done

   readiness reads.

## 15. Navigation menu (agreed 2026-11-03)

Two levels, hiding what does not apply to the viewer:

| Level 1 | Level 2 |
|---|---|
| **My Account** | Profile · Privacy · Referrals |
| **Courses** | the course list |
| **GDCluster / Redgd** | Ranking · Budget (only if the wallet paid the course) · Cluster (only if the pastor paid the course) |
| **learn.tg** | Leaderboard · Transparency · Church Directory · Donate - Lensenia |

  Both checks already exist (`/api/course-catalog`, `/api/cluster/status`); the header queries them
  once per session (one effect, four `fetch`) instead of passing them by context.
- [x] Header implemented (2026-11-06, `apps/web/components/Header.tsx`): the ☰ menu is two levels
      with an accessible disclosure per group (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, one `<ul>` per

      there, **GDCluster / Redgd** only when the server already offers the GD course to that
      profile — the same rule `/[lang]` uses to list it (R-#194) — with Ranking public, Budget
