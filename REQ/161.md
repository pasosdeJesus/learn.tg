## Course Rewards

| Reward | Amount (configured) | Formula (per user) | Notes |
|--------|---------------------|--------------------|-------|
| **USDT** | 1 USDT per guide | `floor(configuredUsdt × profileScore / 100)` | USDT uses 6 decimals internally (1 USDT = 1,000,000); paid from course vault; subject to availability |
| **SLEARN** | 5 SLEARN per guide | `floor(configuredSlearn × profileScore / 100)` | SLEARN uses 2 decimals internally (1 SLEARN = 100); paid from course vault; subject to availability |

**Scholarship split (#196):** When a student earns a scholarship:
- **80%** → student's wallet
- **10%** → referrer (if the student was referred)
- **10%** → cluster fund (or country fund if the student's church is not in a cluster) — sent to `learnTgWallet` and then routed by backend to #155

**Formula detail:** Both amounts are in smallest units. `floor()` integer division. With `profileScore = 50` → 0.5 USDT + 2.5 SLEARN. With `profileScore = 100` → 1 USDT + 5 SLEARN.

**When vault balance is insufficient:** no scholarship is paid for that guide, nothing is deferred and no debt is recorded, and the course does not mark the guide as received. The vault status is visible in the course list: each course card shows three indicators (guides completed, scholarships paid in USDT, scholarships paid in SLEARN); yellow means the vault can pay at least one more guide, gray means it has no funds (`apps/web/lib/course-progress.ts`). A student who wants the scholarship waits until the indicator is yellow and submits the crossword then; guide 3 says exactly this.

**Course price and discounts** (anchored at Sierra Leone 0.50 USDT and the United States 3.00 USDT, with no ceiling):

| Country | HDI | Price (USDT) | Price (SLEARN, 10% discount, rate 22) |
|---------|-----|--------------|----------------------------------------|
| Sierra Leone | 0.467 | 0.50 | 9.90 SLEARN |
| Ghana | 0.628 | 1.35 | 26.73 SLEARN |
| Colombia | 0.788 | 2.20 | 43.56 SLEARN |
| United States | 0.938 | 3.00 | 59.40 SLEARN |
| Other countries | from `m_hdi` | line between the two anchors | same conversion |

The rule lives in `packages/rewards/src/lib/premium-pricing.ts` (`calculatePremiumPriceUsdt`, `calculatePremiumPriceSlearn`) and `premium-purchase` validates the same number server-side.

> **SLEARN price:** it is computed at request time, not stored in a column, from `usdtToSlearnRate` on the contract (adjustable in [10, 22]) with a 10% discount, in both the price endpoint and the purchase validation (`premium-purchase.ts` reads the rate). If the rate cannot be read for a payment that includes SLEARN, the purchase is rejected with 503; a purchase without country or HDI is rejected with 400.

## Country fund and cluster fund release

### Country fund: sources of income

The country fund receives:

1. **10% of GD course sales** when the student's church is in no cluster or its cluster fund was already released.
2. **10% of each guide's scholarship** when the student's church is in no cluster or its cluster fund was already released.
3. **Direct donations** sent to the country from the ranking (`ClusterFundsV2.processCountryDonation`).

The country fund is replenished **only** by those three sources; the 10% of a cluster's own students goes to that cluster's fund, not to the country fund. It is a launch fund for the first clusters, not a permanent source: pdJ distributes it among the verified clusters of the country when it decides (see below). On 2026-10-28 production showed US$51.82 in the Sierra Leone country fund and 1.60 SLEARN in Colombia's.

### Presentation to Global Disciples: states

When the cluster is active with 3+ churches, every principal pastor finished the 6 guides, has `profilescore > 90`, invited at least one pastor/co-pastor and at least one member/leader of their own church (`referralrelationship`), the cluster wallet is set, the first-year budget (`first_year_budget`, `budget_draft_path`) and the director's offer (`director_offer_path`, `director_first_year_pay`, `director_first_year_hours`) are complete, and **every pastor pressed the approve button**, the initiator requests the presentation on the cluster page.

The approval is the pastors' consent: each one confirms that they know and approve what will be sent to GD (the budget and the director's offer) **and the cluster wallet**. It is stored in `cluster_gd_approval` with the SHA-256 of the five fields + the wallet, so if any of them changes the approvals expire by themselves. From the moment of the request the five fields **and the wallet** are fixed (read-only).

The request goes through these states:

| State | Who sets it | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `requested` | The initiator (after every pastor approved) | Budget, director and wallet are fixed (read-only); the request appears in pdJ's queue |
| `under_review` | pdJ | pdJ is reviewing the request |
| `returned` | pdJ | Budget, director and wallet are **unlocked** for the initiator; pdJ's evaluation and suggestions are shown. If the data or the wallet change, the pastors must approve again |
| `approved` | pdJ | Budget, director and wallet are fixed again (read-only) |
| `sent` | pdJ | The message was sent to GD |
| `answered` | pdJ | GD's answer was recorded and sent to the initiator |

**Lock/unlock logic:** entering `requested` or `approved` locks the five fields and the wallet; entering `returned` unlocks them. The approvals are for a specific hash: a change invalidates them and the request stays blocked until every pastor approves again. Every state change is recorded in `clustergd_history` with the actor and the timestamp. The initiator can **cancel** the request before pdJ reviews it (`requested` → `cancelled`, fields unlocked).

**What pdJ does:** pdJ is not GD staff. It reviews the request, writes to its GD contact with the cluster data (the suggested subject and body are shown on screen in `/{lang}/admin/clusters` for pdJ to copy, paste and edit; the app does not store the recipient address), records GD's answer and the email/channel to continue, and marks the request as sent. The app has no mailer, so pdJ sends the email from its own client. The initiator sees the status and GD's answer on the cluster page (in-app notification too).

**Schema.** Reuses the existing `clustergd.gd_requested_*`, `gd_sent_*`, `gd_response*` columns and adds, in migration `20261028140000_cluster_gd_presentation_states.ts`: `gd_status` (CHECK: `requested` | `under_review` | `returned` | `approved` | `sent` | `answered` | `cancelled`), `gd_reviewed_at`, `gd_returned_at`, `gd_approved_at`, `gd_answered_at`, `gd_evaluation`, `gd_suggestions`, the budget and director columns (`first_year_budget`, `budget_draft_path`, `budget_fixed_at`, `director_offer_path`, `director_first_year_pay`, `director_first_year_hours`, `director_fixed_at`), `ranking_exited_at` and the table `cluster_gd_approval` (`clustergd_id`, `usuario_id`, `data_hash`, `created_at`; migration `20261028180000_cluster_gd_approval.ts`). `cluster_fund_vote` is no longer used (there are no withdrawal votes). It also widens `clustergd_history.event_type` from `varchar(20)` to `varchar(40)`: the event `gd_presentation_requested` (25 characters) did not fit and made the request fail at runtime.

**Routes.** `GET|POST|PUT|DELETE /api/cluster/[id]/gd-presentation`: the initiator requests (POST) and cancels while pdJ has not reviewed it (DELETE); pdJ moves the state (`under_review`, `returned`, `approved`, `sent`, `answered`) with its evaluation, suggestions and GD's answer (PUT, admin only). The budget and director data are `PUT /api/cluster/[id]/budget` and `PUT /api/cluster/[id]/director` (initiator, locked while the presentation is fixed), their PDFs go through `POST|GET|DELETE /api/cluster/[id]/document` (stored in `uploads/cluster/{id}/`, never in `public/`), and the pastors' approvals are `GET|POST /api/cluster/[id]/approval`.

**The rules for the student live in guide 6 of the course** (`resources/{en,es}/gdcluster/guide6.md`), which is where they belong; there is no separate doc.

### Country fund → cluster fund

**Verified cluster = presented cluster (operator, 2026-10-22).** The backend calls
`ClusterFundsV2.setClusterVerification(wallet, true)` when pdJ moves the presentation to
`sent` ("presented in the database = verified in the contract"). If the cluster changes its
wallet while `sent` or `answered`, the new wallet is verified too, because the contract flag
is indexed by address. A verified cluster is what `releaseClusterFunds` requires.

**Distribution when GD answers.** When pdJ records GD's answer (`answered`), the backend
takes the clusters of that country whose presentation is `sent`/`answered`, keeps the ones
that exist and are verified on-chain, and calls
`redistributeCountryFunds(countryCode, wallets)`: **the whole balance of the country fund is
split equally among the presented (verified) clusters** and credited to each cluster's fund.
Right after that it calls `releaseClusterFunds` for that cluster, so it receives its own
fund (including its share). The other clusters keep their share in their fund until GD
answers for them. Both calls happen automatically inside the `answered` transition; a
failure is recorded (`country_fund_failed` / `fund_release_failed`) and does not undo the
registered answer. A country fund with no balance,
or no verified cluster on-chain, is a no-op; a failure in the distribution is recorded in the
cluster history (`country_fund_failed`) and does not block the release of the cluster fund.

The country fund keeps receiving the 10% of the churches that are in no cluster (or whose
cluster fund was already released) and the donations sent to the country.

**Proposal (not a blocker; see https://github.com/pasosdeJesus/learn.tg/issues/294):** the
cluster whose presentation GD answers receives **50 % of the country fund**, and the other
50 % stays in the fund, instead of the equal split among the presented clusters. It needs a new contract function (the deployed one only splits or releases the
whole balance), so the equal split is what the app does meanwhile.

### Approval and automatic release

- Each principal pastor of a member church presses the **approve** button on the cluster page; the button is enabled while the request is `null`, `cancelled` or `returned`, and only for a pastor who has not approved the current hash.
- With every approval saved (`cluster_gd_approval`, one row per pastor with the hash), the initiator can request the review. There is no withdrawal vote and no automatic release by budget (decision of the operator, 2026-10-22).
- The wallet is fixed from `requested`; a cluster with a problem between its pastors resolves it with an admin's mediation or by reconfiguring the cluster before requesting.
- When pdJ records `answered`, the backend releases automatically: `redistributeCountryFunds` among the presented/verified clusters and `releaseClusterFunds` for the cluster whose presentation was answered; `fund_released_at` and the amounts are recorded, and from then on the 10% of those churches goes to the country fund. A chain failure is recorded in the history (`fund_release_failed`, `country_fund_failed`) and the answer stays.

### Wallet validation

`PUT /api/cluster/[id]` validates the cluster wallet with `validateClusterWallet` (`packages/gdcluster/src/lib/cluster-wallet.ts`): a valid EVM address with EIP-55 checksum when it comes in mixed case, not the zero address, and not an address of the platform (backend, referrals, churches, treasury, UBI, reserves, verifiers) or the `ClusterFundsV2` contract. The response reports `wallet_checksummed`, so the UI warns when the address arrived all in lowercase (no checksum).

`checkWalletControl` (same file) then proves **who controls it**: if the address is a normal account it must be **one of the wallets registered in the initiator's account** (`billetera_usuario`); if it is a contract, it reads Safe's `getOwners()` and requires **one owner per pastor of the cluster**, each owner being a wallet registered in **that pastor's** account (`owners.length === pastors.length` plus a one-to-one matching, so one pastor cannot hold two seats); a contract that does not expose `getOwners()` is rejected (`400 cluster_wallet_unverified`). Rejections: `cluster_wallet_not_initiator`, `cluster_wallet_safe_without_initiator`, `cluster_wallet_safe_owners`, `cluster_wallet_unverified`. No funds move: `getCode` plus a view call. The wallet cannot be changed while the presentation is `requested`, `under_review`, `approved`, `sent` or `answered` (`409 gd_fields_locked`).

### Ranking and donations

- A cluster **does not leave the ranking** when it is presented to GD; it stays listed
  (unless it is disbanded).
- A presented/verified cluster is shown **in green** in the ranking (the same badge for
  `sent` and `answered`; `requested`/`under_review`/`returned`/`approved` show "in process
  with pdJ" in amber) and **loses the Donate button**: from then on it is funded through the
  country fund distribution, not with donations from the ranking.
- The 10% of its students' purchases and scholarships **keeps going to the cluster fund**
  until the fund is released (`fund_released_at`), which now happens when GD answers.
- `clustergd.ranking_exited_at` is no longer written (there are no withdrawal votes); the
  ranking query does not filter by it.

### Data fixed at the request

The cluster page records two sets of data before the presentation to Global Disciples:

**First-year budget:**
- `clustergd.first_year_budget` (numeric): the fixed total.
- `clustergd.budget_draft_path` (varchar): the PDF of the draft.
- `clustergd.budget_fixed_at` (timestamp): when it was fixed (null while editable). The cluster keeps the fixed total as its plan for the first year; it does not trigger a release and the ranking exit does not depend on it.

**Program Director:**
- `clustergd.director_offer_path` (varchar): PDF with the job offer and the months-vs-functions matrix.
- `clustergd.director_first_year_pay` (numeric): total to pay the director in the first year.
- `clustergd.director_first_year_hours` (numeric): expected working hours in the first year.
- `clustergd.director_fixed_at` (timestamp): when it was fixed (null while editable).

Both sets are editable until the initiator requests the presentation (`requested`). At that moment, they are fixed (read-only), together with the cluster wallet. If pdJ returns the request (`returned`), they and the wallet are unlocked until the initiator corrects them and pdJ approves them again (`approved`); if any of the data or the wallet changes, the pastors' approvals expire and must be collected again. The request is rejected if any of the five fields is missing, the wallet is not set or a pastor has not approved.

**Other cluster state:**
- `clustergd.ranking_exited_at` (timestamp): no longer written (there are no withdrawal votes).
- `clustergd.fund_released_at` (timestamptz): when the fund was released to the wallet (null while accumulating); it is also the signal that the 10% of those churches now goes to the country fund.
- `clustergd.fund_released_amount` / `clustergd.fund_released_slearn` (numeric): the USDT and SLEARN amounts released.
- Approvals are recorded in `cluster_gd_approval` (`clustergd_id`, `usuario_id`, `data_hash`, `created_at`, unique on the pair), so "X of Y pastors approved" is a query and a change of data invalidates the approvals.

## Inconsistencies found (and what was done)

1. **`clustergd_history.event_type` was `varchar(20)` and `gd_presentation_requested` is 25 characters.** Requesting the presentation inserted that event, so the POST would fail with `value too long for type character varying(20)` on a real database (the unit test mocks the DB). Fixed: the column is `varchar(40)`.
2. **Guides 3-6 describe features that did not exist**: the first-year budget (PDF + total), the director's offer (PDF + pay + hours), the fixed/read-only lock, the six presentation states, the pastors' approval and the automatic release. Implemented as described above.
3. **What happens to the 10 % when a cluster leaves the ranking.** Three versions coexisted: the requirement said it continued to the cluster, guide 3 said it stopped and went to the country fund, and the code sent it to the cluster forever. Corrected on 2026-10-22 with the rule the operator defined: the 10 % keeps going to the cluster fund while the fund exists (the churches are still in the cluster), and it goes to the country fund **only after the fund is released** to the cluster wallet (`fund_released_at`). `resolveGDClusterDestination` now uses `fund_released_at`, not `ranking_exited_at`.
4. **The country fund rule changed several times**: from "50 % on `answered`" (impossible with `ClusterFundsV2`) to "equal redistribution among verified clusters" (needs verification to happen before completion) to "the first cluster that completes takes the whole balance" (implemented and then reverted, because it punished the other presented clusters) and finally to the operator's decision: **"presented" (`sent`) = verified in the contract**, so several clusters can be verified at once and pdJ distributes the country fund equally among them; a completing cluster only receives its own fund. Guides, code and this requirement are aligned with that. R-#294 keeps the proposal to change it to 50 % per cluster once the contract can move a percentage.
5. **`gd_status` vs the old columns**: clusters that had already requested or sent the presentation are backfilled (`requested`/`sent`/`answered`) so guide 6's status view has a value.
6. **Header drift**: the REQ still named `GdPastoresLanding` as a component of the engine; the landing is `PastorLanding` in the core (R-#283). Corrected.
7. **Spanish guides were not a translation of the English ones**: `guia4` was the program director, `guia5` was an Aave guide ("Ahorra mientras esperas") and there was no budget guide; `guia6` said "la plataforma no prepara el presupuesto local: un presupuesto pertenece a una etapa posterior", which contradicts guides 4-5. The Aave guide left this course; its content is still in the Spanish submodule history (`e65bc51`) if the operator wants it as a separate course.

## Accessibility: Reaching Pastors Without Smartphones or Literacy

Sierra Leone has pastors and church leaders who cannot read well, do not own a smartphone, or both. The platform must not exclude them. These are Phase 2+ ideas for making the course accessible beyond the digital-native audience.

### In-Person + Digital Hybrid

| Scenario | Solution |
|----------|----------|
| Pastor with smartphone takes course | Standard flow — self-service on learn.tg |
| Pastor without smartphone but in a group | **Facilitator mode:** one smartphone shared among 3-5 pastors. Facilitator reads guides aloud, group discusses, facilitator marks crossword answers. One wallet per group or per pastor (wallet can be on facilitator's device). |
| Pastor without smartphone, alone | **Printed materials:** PDF/printable version of each guide (~1 page, 400-700 words). Pastor studies offline, then visits a facilitator or cybercafé to complete the crossword and claim rewards. |
| Pastor who cannot read | **Audio guides:** each guide recorded as 3-5 minute audio (English, Krio, Mende, Temne). Listen on shared smartphone, radio, or MP3 player. |

### Technical Enablers (Phase 2+)

| Feature | Description | Priority |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| **Audio recordings** | MP3 per guide, downloadable, playable offline. Hosted on learn.tg alongside text. | High |
| **Printable PDF** | Each guide as a single-page PDF with the crossword at the bottom. Generatable from the markdown source. | High |
| **Group/facilitator mode** | One authenticated user can mark progress for multiple pastors (name + wallet per pastor). SBTs and rewards go to each pastor's wallet. | Medium |
| **Offline crossword** | Complete crossword offline, sync when back online. Already partially supported by the existing crossword system. | Medium |
| **WhatsApp delivery** | Guide text + crossword questions delivered via WhatsApp bot. Pastor responds with answers, bot records progress. | Low |
| **Radio/podcast distribution** | Audio guides distributed via community radio stations or SD cards for MP3 players. | Low |

### Access Control (https://github.com/pasosdeJesus/learn.tg/issues/194)

The Global Disciples course is restricted to churches whose position on Israel/Gaza is not unconditional support (`usuario.position_israel_gaza = 'no'`). The check lives in `lib/course-access.ts` and is consumed by `GET /api/courses/[courseId]/purchase-eligibility` and by `/api/guide`; the course list and the course page hide the course from everybody else.

| Value | Access |
|-------|--------|
| `'no'` | Granted |
| `'yes'` | Denied — neutral message: "This course is not available for your profile yet." / "Este curso aún no está disponible para tu perfil." |
| `NULL` | Denied — the same neutral message (the position must be declared) |

The denial text is mapped from the machine key `gd_non_zionist` by `lib/course-access-msg.ts`. The condition is never announced publicly (R-#283).
