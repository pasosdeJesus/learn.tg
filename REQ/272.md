# R-#272: Redesign list of courses /en y /es

**Link:** https://github.com/pasosdeJesus/learn.tg/issues/272

**Status:** Proposal (this file replaces the 2026-10-01 stub)
**Created:** 2026-10-01
**Type:** Enhancement (UI/UX)
**Priority:** Medium — the course list is the entry point of the whole site, but nothing is broken; the current cards are just crowded.

---

## 1. Problem

The course list (`apps/nextjs/app/[lang]/page.tsx:255-377`) renders one tall card per course inside a three-column grid. Each card shows:

- the course image (`:283-293`, `h-[17rem]`),
- the **title** and the **subtítulo** (`:294-301`),
- the compact `CourseStatistics` block (`:302-322`) — at `full={false}` this still prints the scholarship line, the cooldown/eligibility line and **three progress bars** (`✅` guides, `💵` USDT, `⚡` SLEARN, `components/CourseStatistics.tsx:136-167`),
- an "Ir al curso" / "Go to course" button styled as a blue span (`:323-329`),
- and `CourseDonation` when the vault exists (`:332-342`).

With every card carrying a paragraph of numbers plus three 4-px-tall bars plus a full-width button plus a donation row, the list reads as a wall of text: the learner cannot see, at a glance, **which courses are progressing and which are rewarded**. The operator asked for compact cards that show only **image + title + short description**, with the progress reduced to **three small circles** whose arcs carry the meaning, and for a line stating **how many courses** the list contains.

## 2. Objective

1. A compact course card: **image + title + short description + the "Ir al curso" button** (the explicit CTA is kept: it is the intuitive affordance on a phone) and nothing else (no scholarship paragraph, no cooldown/eligibility line, no `CourseDonation` row).
2. Three **small progress circles** attached to the **card frame** (on the border, consuming no extra vertical space — or, at worst, the smallest possible footer), one per signal, each with the icon already used by the bars:
   - **check** — guides completed out of the course total;
   - **dollar** — USDT scholarships paid out of the total;
   - **SLEARN** (`/img/slearn-icon.svg`) — SLEARN scholarships paid out of the total.
3. Colour carries the state, per signal (a ring around each icon, one colour each):
   - **check** — green when every guide is completed, yellow while some guide is not (never gray: a pending guide can always be completed);
   - **USDT / SLEARN** — green when every scholarship of that token was claimed, yellow while some remain unclaimed **and** the vault can pay one now, gray while some remain unclaimed and nothing is available now.
   This is exactly the meaning the cards' bars keep in the course summary (`CourseStatistics`, R-#252), so the list and the detail never disagree.
4. A **course count** line in the list header (e.g. "6 courses") so the learner knows the size of the catalogue.
5. The **course landing page** (`/${idioma}${prefijoRuta}`) keeps its full summary unchanged: the progress bars with their numbers, percentages and icons, the total available, the total paid, the eligibility/cooldown line and the **donate button**. The list is the only place that gets compact.

## 3. Design

### 3.1 The card

```
┌───────────────────────────────┐
│          [ course image ]     │
│                               │
│  Title                        │
│  Short description (2 lines)  │
│                               │
│        [ Ir al curso ▸ ]      │
└───┬───────┬───────┬───────────┘
    ✅○     💵◔     ⚡●            ← three circles on the card frame
```

- The whole card keeps being a single link to `/${idioma}${prefijoRuta}` (today the `<a>` wraps image, header and footer), and the "Ir al curso" button **stays** inside it.
- Remove from the card: the compact `CourseStatistics` block and the `CourseDonation` row. The explicit CTA is not what crowds the card; the paragraph of numbers and the donation row are.
- The description keeps `line-clamp` (2 lines) so cards stay the same height.
- The three circles are **attached to the card frame**: they straddle the bottom border (a small negative vertical margin over the border line) so they consume no height and a phone can show several courses at once. They stay small (`size ≈ 34-40`, `strokeWidth ≈ 5-6`: the arc is deliberately thick, it wraps the icon like a ring). If straddling the border complicates the hover/focus styles or the grid, the fallback is a compact footer row of the same size — still the smallest footprint possible.

### 3.2 The three circles

Each circle reuses the geometry of `components/ui/completed-progress.tsx` (`CombinedCircularProgress`: SVG `circle` with `strokeDasharray`/`strokeDashoffset`, rotated `-90deg`), but with its own single colour, its own icon in the middle and no percentage text:

| Circle | Icon | Arc = | Full arc when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guides | check (`✅` / SVG) | `completedGuides / totalGuides` | all guides of the course are completed |
| USDT | `💵` (or a dollar SVG) | `paidGuidesUSDT / totalGuides` | the course's guides are all paid in USDT |
| SLEARN | `/img/slearn-icon.svg` | `paidGuidesSLEARN / totalGuides` | the course's guides are all paid in SLEARN |

The per-guide figures come from `/api/scholarship?courseId=<id>` and are already on the page (`app/[lang]/page.tsx:158-173`, `CourseExtra`). No API change is expected.

### 3.3 Colour rules

| Signal | Green | Yellow | Gray |
|---|---|---|---|
| **check** | every guide is completed (`completed === total`) | some guide is not completed | — never (a pending guide can always be completed) |
| **USDT** | every guide is paid in USDT (`paid === total`) | `paid < total` **and** `available > 0`, where `available = min(floor(vaultBalance / amountPerGuide), total - paid)` (`CourseStatistics.tsx:151-153`) | `paid < total` **and** `available === 0` |
| **SLEARN** | every guide is paid in SLEARN (`paid === total`) | `paid < total` **and** `available > 0`, where `available = min(floor(vaultBalanceSlearn / amountPerGuideSlearn), total - paid)` (`CourseStatistics.tsx:160-162`) | `paid < total` **and** `available === 0` |

This is exactly what the cards' bars already mean (`CourseStatistics`): the filled (green) segment is what was achieved, the yellow segment is the headroom available right now, and no yellow means nothing can move the circle. The three circles and the summary bars must never disagree.

The background ring stays gray so the "empty" part of every circle reads as the remaining work.

**Offline (R-#240, R-#256):** the live vault balance is unavailable, so USDT and SLEARN report only **green** (every guide already paid in that token) or **gray** (anything else — never yellow, because availability cannot be established). The check circle is unaffected (green if all guides are completed, yellow otherwise). Today the list leaves `extCourses` empty offline (`app/[lang]/page.tsx:189-197`), so the implementation must persist the last known per-course counts for the circles to have something to show; if it does not, they stay hidden and the card is just image + title + description.

### 3.4 List header

Above the grid, next to `OfflineDownloadAll` (`app/[lang]/page.tsx:269`), a line with the number of courses in the catalogue for the current language, EN/ES (`"6 courses"` / `"6 cursos"`).

## 4. Scope

**In scope**

- `apps/nextjs/app/[lang]/page.tsx` — compact card that keeps the "Ir al curso" button, the three circles attached to the card frame, and the header count.
- A new small component for the three circles (e.g. `components/CourseProgressDots.tsx`) built on the arc geometry of `components/ui/completed-progress.tsx`, plus a **pure helper** that maps `{ completedGuides, totalGuides, paidGuidesUSDT, paidGuidesSLEARN, vaultBalance, vaultBalanceSlearn, amountPerGuide, amountPerGuideSlearn }` to `{ ratio, state: 'done' | 'available' | 'blocked', colorClass }` per signal.
- Unit tests for the helper (boundaries: 0 guides, all completed, `available` cap, no vault, division by zero) and a component test for the circles.
- EN and ES strings for the header count.

**Out of scope**

- Any change to `/api/scholarship`, the vault, the payouts or the DB.
- The `full` statistics view (`CourseStatistics` with `full={true}`), which keeps its current detail and its bars.
- The course landing pages: `/${idioma}${prefijoRuta}` keeps its **full** summary untouched — the progress bars with numbers, percentages and icons, the total available, the total paid, the scholarship/eligibility/cooldown lines and the donate button (`CourseStatistics full={true}` and `CourseDonation` stay as they are).
- `CourseDonation` itself and the donation flow (only its removal from the list card).
- The `SlearnInfo` block below the grid (`:347-375`).

## 6. Testing plan

- **Unit:** the pure helper (all states and boundaries) and a component test that asserts one SVG arc per signal, the icon per signal and the colour class per state.
- **E2E (Puppeteer, dev site):** load `/en` and `/es`, assert the count line, assert every card has three circles with the expected icons and the "Ir al curso" button and no `✅`/`💵`/`⚡` progress bars, and assert the card (and the button) still navigate to the course.
- **Manual (operator):** visual check with a wallet that has completed guides and paid scholarships (the `.env` wallet) to confirm the three colours.

## 7. Risks

- **Decided (was an open question):** each circle tells its own story. Green means *that* signal's goal was reached (check = all guides completed; USDT = all guides paid in USDT; SLEARN = all guides paid in SLEARN), never "all guides completed turns everything green". Resolved by the operator on 2026-10-01 and aligned with the summary bars (`CourseStatistics`).
- "Available now" depends on the vault balance, which the page refreshes with a 6-second countdown after a donation (`app/[lang]/page.tsx:70-86`): the circles must re-read the same state, not cache it.
- The arcs must not imply money the learner cannot get: `available` caps at `totalGuides - paid`, the existing rule.
- The circles sit on the card frame: the hover/focus styles and the grid must survive the negative margin, and the button keeps the explicit "Go to course" affordance. Accessibility: the card stays an `<a>`; add an `aria-label` on each circle summarising its counts (colour alone is not a signal).
