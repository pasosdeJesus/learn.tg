**Finding 2026-10-28 — ambiguous network failure on a premium purchase (operator report).**
Purchasing from a new account showed viem's `An unknown RPC error occurred … Details: Failed to
fetch` while sending the USDT `transfer` (`eth_sendTransaction` through the wallet provider, so the
failing endpoint is the wallet's RPC, or the app RPC for the in-app wallet — a single
`NEXT_PUBLIC_RPC_URL` with no fallback). The retry succeeded minutes later.

- **Status 2026-10-28:** the two silent fallbacks of the pricing path are gone (a
  SLEARN payment without a readable `usdtToSlearnRate` answers **503**, and a purchase
  without country/HDI answers **400** instead of using 2 USDT), and the `admin_solves`
  queue now has a verifier page and API, so an unrecorded 10% is visible and can be
  closed with a note (see the `admin_solves` bullet and the changes of 2026-10-28 in R-#161).
- **Money risk (open):** `Failed to fetch` does **not** prove the transaction was not broadcast.
  If the wallet did send it, the retry can produce a **second transfer** that the backend does not
  reconcile with the purchase (it verifies the hash it is given), i.e. funds paid without a recorded
  purchase. Mitigation today is only the message; a real fix needs either (a) an idempotency key per
  purchase attempt, or (b) reading the wallet's pending transaction / nonce before allowing a retry.
  Worth deciding together with the in-app wallet (m work items 14/37): a `fallback([...])`
  transport with a second public RPC would remove most of the transient failures at the source.
- **UX fixed (2026-10-28):** `handlePay` of `CheckoutModal` and `handleDonateClick` of
  `DonateModal` now call the payment hook's `reset()` before each attempt, so a previous error no
  longer stays on screen while the new attempt runs (the engine's `execute` does not clear it); the
  modal explains the network/RPC case in the student's language, keeps the technical detail for
  copying, and shows what is being waited on ("confirm in your wallet" / "waiting for the network").
  Tests: `CheckoutModal.light.test.tsx` (9).

**Finding 2026-10-28 — per-course vault balances are bookkeeping over one pooled balance.**
Reported unsolicited by KupiaSec (code read, no PoC, no test against the deployment). Verified
read-only on Celo mainnet, block ≈ 81 410 000. **Forensics (added the same day and completed with
the V2 logs, all read-only):** the shortfall is the retired course 106 ("W e b 3 e IBU
--retirado"), which carries **8 USDT of bookkeeping with no tokens behind it** in V3
(`0x34b0…4728`), V4 (`0x42dc…0479`) and V5; V3 and V4 hold 0 tokens and V5 holds the 13.19.
The 8 USDT **were real and did enter the fund**; it was a test deposit into a course that was
**already retired**, and when the mistake was noticed the owner withdrew the 8 USDT that had
stayed in the vault **1 min 41 s later**, leaving the books untouched. That is the origin of the
gap, not the migrations: they only copied the number forward.

- **2026-01-13 21:04:31 UTC, block 56 437 513, tx `0xb2440af7…d29162b`**: the owner wallet
  `0x3586…39A7` called `deposit(106, 10000000)` on **V2** (`0x4AE3…33D7`). The contract moved
  `2.000000` USDT (20 % team fee) to the owner and `8.000000` USDT (80 %) to itself, and
  recorded `vaults(106).balanceUsdt = 8.000000`. The `Deposit` event shows the gross 10 USDT
  (`emit Deposit(courseId, amount)` before the split, `LearnTGVaults.sol:141`), which is why
  the log alone looks like the vault received 10.
- **2026-01-13 21:06:12 UTC, block 56 437 614, tx `0x6f22cc3a…c548600e0e2`**: the owner
  called `emergencyWithdrawUsdt(8000000)` on V2 and got **exactly the 8 USDT back**, after
  noticing the deposit into an already-retired course. The course kept its 8 USDT on the books
  because V2 accepted the deposit and had no call to zero a balance: that is the moment the gap
  was born.
- **2026-06-02 06:19:08 UTC, block 68 480 390, tx `0x101de78b…c6284ab`**: the owner called
  `emergencyWithdrawUsdt(8248137)` on V2. The contract emitted
  `EmergencyWithdrawal(USDT, 8248137)` and transferred **8.248137 USDT** to `0x3586…39A7`,
  leaving V2 with 0 USDT (it still holds 0). That function is a plain
  `usdtToken.transfer(owner, amount)` and does **not** touch `vaults[].balanceUsdt`
  (`LearnTGVaults.sol:284-289`). Course 106 kept its 8 USDT on the books while the tokens left
  the contract (and came back into the fund 3 s later, see the next point); the extra 0.248137
  was the rest of what V2 held that day.
- **The withdrawal was the first half of a migration, not money leaving the fund:** 3 s later
  (block 68 480 393, tx `0x846f46d8…31171ff`, 06:19:11) the owner sent **exactly
  8.248137 USDT** from `0x3586…39A7` to **V3** (`0x34b0…4728`). The pattern repeats in every
  migration: V3 `emergencyWithdrawUSDT(11.418021)` on 2026-06-09 18:43:36 (block 69 129 858)
  followed 5 s later by a transfer of 11.418021 to V4, and V4 `0x1d7fa77f(12.224915)` on
  2026-08-12 14:33:57 (block 74 644 479) followed by 12.224915 to V5 (block 74 644 485). The
  scholarship fund has never lost a unit of USDT this way.
- V3, V4 and V5 inherited the number through `setVaultBalance(106, 8000000, 0)` (for V5,
  **2026-08-12 14:36:25 UTC**, block 74 644 627) because the migrations copy the books and
  `setVaultBalance` only checks each amount against the whole balance, never the sum. No token
  ever moved to V5 for course 106 and there is no `CourseFundsRecorded` event for it.
- **The books of each version, read today** (the `vaults` mapping survives the migrations):
  V2 16.248137 vs 8.248137 held at its sweep (8.000000 over), V3 19.418021 vs 11.418021
  (8.000000), V4 20.265825 vs 12.224915 (8.040910), V5 20.262753 declared against the
  12.224915 received (8.037838). So every migration copied the phantom faithfully. The
  0.040910 on top of the 8 is not an emergency withdrawal (V4 has only the two of the
  2026-08-12 migration, and the USDT one went 1:1 to V5): it comes from running the V3→V4
  migration **twice**, because `setVaultBalance` is not idempotent. The second run
  (2026-06-10 09:10:32) re-applied the snapshot and undid a record of 0.50909 (course 103), a
  record of 0.21 (course 107) and a payout of 0.76 (course 2), netting 0.040910. V6 rejects
  that second run (`_assertSolvent`) and the runbook forbids it.
- Today the pool is fully claimed by the other courses: without course 106 the books claim
  **13.236402** against **13.195492** held (0.040910), so the 8 USDT of 106 are not parked in
  any wallet; they are a phantom entry on top of a pool that is otherwise allocated. This is the
  hazard itself: the per-course numbers are bookkeeping over one pooled balance, and a retired
  course can keep a claim while the pool pays the others.

So the tokens existed, entered the fund, went back to the owner when the course was retired,
and the only thing that broke is the book of a course that no longer exists. **Corrections to
earlier revisions of this note:** "no `EmergencyWithdrawal` event ever" was read from the
V3/V4/V5 logs instead of V2's; "the 8 USDT sit in the operator's wallet" ignored that the June
sweep was re-deposited into V3 the same minute; and "the gap was written by the V5 setup" was
wrong about the origin: V5 only copied a book that was already broken since the January
withdrawal. Settling figures:

- The scholarship vault is `LearnTGVaultsV5` at **0x4d1607A8AEfCdF7B24208E5ceA1e55085781Fc3B**
  (`VERSION() = 5`), the address in `apps/contracts/deployments/LearnTGVaults/V5/celo.json` and
  the one `SLEARN.learnTGVault()` returns; its `usdtToken`/`slearnToken` are the Celo USDT and
  SLEARN constants of `@pasosdejesus/mpdj` (that package does **not** publish the vault address).
- `SLEARN.learnTGVault == learnTGVaultSLEARN` on mainnet, so the "USDT to one contract, SLEARN
  minted to another" hazard exists in the code (two setters, both amounts recorded on the first)
  but **is not live**; it is a deployment-config risk only.
- `payScholarship` checks a **course's** recorded balance against the contract's **whole**
  balance, never the sum. Summed over every course with `vaults(id).exists` (1, 2, 10, 101, 103,
  104, 105, 106, 107): recorded **21.236402 USDT** vs held **13.195492** (gap −8.040910) and
  recorded **819.23 SLEARN** vs held **834.29** (+15.06). Course 106 ("retired", per-guide
  0.000001) accounts for 8 USDT of the 8.040910; the remaining 0.040910 is 0.037838 that the V5
  setup declared above the tokens received plus 0.003072 of rounding over the year, and stays
  inside the built-in tolerance of the check.
- Mechanism that opens a gap **without a payment bug**: `setVaultBalance` (the migration helper)
  can declare more than the vault holds, and `emergencyWithdrawUSDT/SLEARN` are `onlyOwner` and
  do not adjust `vaults[].balance*`, so a sweep that is not matched by a deposit in the next
  vault would leave the same phantom. `recordCourseFunds` adds what it receives and
  `payScholarship` deducts exactly what it transfers, so the gap does not come from payments.
- `ClusterFundsV2`: `setFeeRecipients` requires Σ fees ≤ 100 and `setDonorCashbackPct` ≤ 50,
  validated **separately**; `_distributeFees` subtracts both from the donation with checked
  arithmetic, so a configuration of 100 + 10 makes the whole `processDonation` /
  `processCountryDonation` revert (atomic: nothing moves, donations fail until the owner
  corrects the configuration). Today it is 10 + 10.
- SLEARN purchases depend on `learnTgReserve` holding the USDT and on its allowance: if either is
  missing, `processPayment` with SLEARN reverts (no loss, but purchases fail). `recordCourseFunds`
  also reverts `processPayment` when the course has no vault, so the vault must exist before
  selling.

**Tooling (2026-10-28):** `apps/web/bin/check-vault-balances.mjs` (target `make vault-check`)
prints the per-course table and the sum/real comparison, warns when the two SLEARN vault
addresses differ and exits 1 when the gap exceeds its **built-in** tolerance (0.05 USDT /
0.50 SLEARN, deliberately not a flag: a parameter would be raised until the check passes).
Documented in `doc/runbook.md` §4, which now makes the check **mandatory after any
`setVaultBalance` or `emergencyWithdraw*`**.

**Decision on the contract (2026-10-28, confirmed 2026-10-22):** V5 is not redeployed for
this. `setVaultBalance` is an `onlyOwner` migration helper used a few times per version, and
redeploying is riskier than the helper. **One contract per course was considered and discarded
as impractical** (deploy and migration per course, a `courseId -> vault` registry, more
addresses to verify); the fix adopted is to do the accounting well in the contract: the
`totalRecordedUsdt/Slearn` accumulators checked by every setter and by `emergencyWithdraw*`, a
`retireCourse(courseId, sendToOwner)` that retires a course, sweeps what is backed to the owner
and zeroes its books in one call, `accountingState()`
for the off-chain check, and `accountingBroken` when a forced withdrawal is unavoidable. It is
**written and tested** in `apps/contracts/contracts/next/LearnTGVaultsV6.sol` +
`apps/contracts/test/next/LearnTGVaultsV6.test.ts` (12 tests, full contract suite 92 tests in 5 files green) and
described in [doc/LearnTGVaultsV6.md](../doc/LearnTGVaultsV6.md); deploying it is the
operator's decision. `make vault-check` already accepts V6 and cross-checks its
`accountingState()`. Until it is deployed the invariant is enforced by procedure (runbook) plus
the script.
