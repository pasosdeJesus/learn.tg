**Link:** https://github.com/pasosdeJesus/learn.tg/issues/297
**Created:** 2026-11-07
**Updated:** 2026-11-15
**Status:** Proposal — content pass plus the page gaps of §3. The budget policy of §4.2 is
decided; §8 lists what is still open.
**Type:** Content (the six guides, both languages) + the private and public cluster pages as the
place where the course is practised
**Priority:** Medium — the course ships; this pass makes it teach what the product does.

## 1. Objective

Two things, one contract:

1. **The page is the course.** Everything the six guides ask the pastor to *do* must be doable on
   `/{lang}/cluster` (and on the public `/{lang}/clusters/[slug]`), and each guide must say where.
   §2 is that map; §3 closes the gaps it reveals.
2. **One theme per guide.** Each guide owns a theme, carries a diagram where a picture explains
   faster, and links to the guide that owns everything else instead of repeating it (§4).

## 3. Gaps to close

1. **Guide 2, the 6-character code.** Remove it from the text and from its comprehension question
   (Q4): the product deleted the code in R-#299. The guide must teach the two real ways to invite —
   the **candidate list** (referrals) and the **wallet** of a verified pastor.
2. **Guide 2, "before you invite".** One short checklist: account, in-app wallet, verified profile,
   Web3&UBI + A relationship with Jesus, GD course paid with the 22 SLEARN bonus. It is the order the
   product already enforces through its candidate filter.
3. **Evaluation and suggestions are painted in four cards.** `ClusterPresentationPanel` renders that
   block outside the `section` guard, so a returned request repeats it under Budget, Director,
   Approvals and Present to GD. Show it once, in **Present to Global Disciples**.
4. **The release is invisible from `/cluster`.** After `answered`, the private page should show the
   release (date, amounts, transaction) with what `GET /api/clusters/[slug]` already reads, so the
   course does not send the pastor to the public page.
5. **Donate from the public page** (decided: yes). `/{lang}/clusters/[slug]` now has a **Donate to
   the cluster fund** button fed by `donation_wallet` from that API: the wallet is published only
   while donations are accepted (type-1 country, fund not released, cluster not presented) and it
   reuses `DonateModal` (80 % fund / 10 % pdJ / 10 % cashback). Guide 3 points there.
6. **The budget the course talks about is the fourth year's.** See §4.2: make it consistent in
   guides 4 and 6 (and their questions); the page's card is the reference.

## 4. Content changes

### 4.1 One theme per guide

| Guide | Owns | Links out instead of explaining |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What Global Disciples is (academy, three programmes, three-year cycle, terms) | cluster → 2, money → 4 |
| 2 | Form the cluster: who, the two invitation ways, the platform, the checklist | fund → 3, budget → 4, presentation → 6 |
| 3 | Make the fund grow: four sources, ranking, donors, referrals | budget → 4, presentation → 6 |
| 4 | **Plan the money**: the fourth-year budget, line items, worked example, stipend, four-year curve | the drafting form → R-#296 |
| 5 | The program director: role, selection, development, hours and cost | budget → 4 |
| 6 | Present to GD: readiness, the five fixed data, the request, GD's answer, release | budget → 4, fund → 3 |

Diagrams (one or two per guide, SVG under `apps/web/public/img/gdcluster/`, with alt text and an
explanation line): the three-year cycle (1); three churches and the two invitation ways (2); the four
sources with their percentages (3); the budget lines and the four-year subsidy curve (4); the
director's year (5); the pipeline `requested → under_review → returned/approved → sent → answered`
with who moves each step (6).

Links between guides are absolute for the guide's language (`/en/gdcluster/guide4`,
`/es/redgd/guia4`), never to the other language.

### 4.2 The budget is the fourth year's; the first years are cheaper for the student

Decided (2026-11-15):

- The number the cluster must plan is the **fourth-year budget**: Global Disciples covers the
  **45 / 30 / 15 / 0 %** curve, so from year 4 the academy stands on its own. That is the amount the
  page asks for (`clustergd.fourth_year_budget`) and the one the pastors approve and pdJ sends with
  the presentation.
- In the **first years the students should pay less**, with the difference covered by Global
  Disciples: a lower price is what fills the first cohorts and builds the good will the academy
  needs. The guide must say so with the arithmetic, instead of presenting a flat price for four
  years.
- Guide 4 therefore teaches: the line items, the worked example, the **four-year curve** (what GD
  covers each year, what students pay, what the cluster must raise) and the **fourth-year total** as
  the goal; guide 6 ties the pastors' approval to that budget.

## 6. New automated check

`apps/web/lib/__tests__/guides-content.test.ts` fails on: fewer than 8 questions or a malformed
answer; an inter-guide link to a guide that does not exist (per language); an image missing under
`apps/web/public/img/`; EN/ES with a different number of sections or questions; and the stale
"6-character code" reappearing in any guide. It prints the word count per guide.
