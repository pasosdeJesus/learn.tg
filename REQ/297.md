## 1. Objective

Give each of the six guides of the Global Disciples course **one theme**, **diagrams** where a
picture explains faster than a paragraph, and **fewer repeated passages**: what two guides say
twice becomes a short sentence plus a **link to the guide that owns it**.

The course keeps its six guides (they are rows in the database and the crossword/credential flow
depends on them); what changes is what each one carries.

## 3. What changes

### 3.1 One theme per guide

Proposed boundary (to be confirmed before writing):

| Guide | Owns | Links out instead of explaining |
|-------|------|--------------------------------|
| 1 | What Global Disciples is and how it works (academy, three programs, the three-year cycle, terms) | cluster mechanics → 2, money → 4 |
| 2 | Form the cluster: people, the three steps, the two codes, the platform | fund → 3, budget → 4, presentation → 6 |
| 3 | Make the fund grow: the four sources, the ranking, why to invite | budget detail → 4, presentation → 6 |
| 4 | Plan the money: academy plan, line items, worked example, stipend, four-year projection | the form that drafts it → R-#296 |
| 5 | The program director: role, selection, development, hours and cost | budget → 4 |
| 6 | Present to GD: readiness, the five fixed data, the request, GD's answer, follow-up | budget → 4, fund → 3 |

Anything that does not fit the guide's theme is **moved to the guide that owns it**, or turned
into one sentence plus a link.

### 3.2 Diagrams

- Diagrams are **images** referenced from the markdown (`![alt](/img/... "title")`), the only form
  the guide renderer and the offline download already handle
  (`lib/offline-course-download.ts` → `guideImageUrls`).
- Proposed set (one or two per guide, no decoration): the GD three-year cycle (1); the cluster
  shape with three churches and the two codes (2); the four sources of the fund with their
  percentages (3); the budget lines and the four-year subsidy curve (4); the director's year (5);
  the presentation pipeline (`requested → under_review → returned/approved → sent → answered`) with
  who moves each step (6).
- Each diagram carries **alt text** and a one-line explanation in the body, so it still teaches
  when the image does not load.
- Files live under `apps/web/public/img/gdcluster/` as **SVG** (small, sharp, translatable text).

### 3.3 Less repetition, more links

- A topic explained once has one owner (table in §3.1); the other guides carry a sentence with a
  link to it.
- Link convention: the guide is a file per language, so the link is written **absolute** for that
  file, e.g. `/en/gdcluster/guide4` in the English guide and `/es/redgd/guia4` in the Spanish one.
  A link never points to a guide of the other language.
- The ritual sections ("Action", "Comprehension Questions") stay in every guide: they are the
  learning contract, not repetition.

### 3.4 Length

Bring each guide to **≤ 700 words** where the objective fits and, in any case, to a single theme.
Guide 3 is expected to shrink the most (its budget and presentation sections leave). The count is
measured and reported, not enforced as a gate.

### 3.5 Questions

- **8 or more** per guide, the same count in both languages, with the exact format of
  `doc/guide-writing.md` (`___` in the body, answer in the **last** parentheses, one numbered item
  per question): that is the contract the crossword uses to grade, including offline.
- When a topic leaves a guide its questions leave with it (the bank of the owning guide grows), so
  no answer is lost and every guide keeps enough variety for retakes (8 questions → 56 puzzles).

## 5. New automated check

`apps/web/lib/__tests__/guides-content.test.ts` reads `resources/{en,es}/{course}/` and fails on:

- a guide with fewer than 8 questions, or a question that breaks the `(answer)` format;
- an inter-guide link that points to a guide file that does not exist (per language);
- a guide whose images do not exist under `apps/web/public/img/`;
- EN/ES with a different number of `## ` sections or questions;
- a `REQ/<n>` reference (must be the issue URL, per `AGENTS.md` §8).

It also prints the word count per guide, so the next content pass starts from numbers instead of
counting by hand.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each guide has one theme and no section that belongs to another guide.
- [ ] Every repeated topic has a single owner, and the other guides link to it.
- [ ] All inter-guide links open the right guide in both languages.
- [ ] Each guide has at least one diagram with alt text and an explanation line.
- [ ] EN and ES carry the same sections and the same number of questions.
- [ ] Every guide has 8+ questions in the exact format; its crossword builds and is solvable.
- [ ] `guide-citations`, `crossword-submit-ends`, `offline-course-download` and `cluster-ready`
      (which solves the six guides of each pastor) stay green.
- [ ] The new `guides-content` check passes and reports the word count per guide.
- [ ] Word counts are recorded in this requirement when the pass is done.
