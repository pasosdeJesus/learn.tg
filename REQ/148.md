# R-#148: SLEARN Advanced Features (Post-Launch)"

Post-launch SLEARN features: burning (`burn`) of SLEARN upon payment, automated referral rewards, SLEARN ↔ Leone (SLE) swapping (stable-sl), church distribution improvements, an advanced transparency dashboard, and governance. Premium course payments are covered by REQ #128.

## ⏳ Dependencies
- Issue #129 completed (SLEARN and LearnTGVaultsV4 on mainnet, initial mint done).

## 🚧 Pending tasks

- [x] Covered by REQ #128 (dynamic pricing, mixed USDT/SLEARN payment, distribution, `premium_course_usuario`, checkout UI).
- [x] `SLEARN.burn` of the SLEARN paid on purchase (implementado dentro de `SLEARN.processPayment` → `_pullTokens` quema los SLEARN del usuario y libera USDT de la reserva).
