### 3. Referral Program (two forms)

The referrer is always paid **from the referral wallet**
(`NEXT_PUBLIC_REFERRAL_WALLET_ADDRESS`), never from scholarships. If the wallet
has no funds, the referrer receives nothing — this is documented behaviour.

**Form 1 — course purchases (all premium courses).**
The referrer earns 10% of the course price when a referred user buys a course.
- On-chain: `SLEARN.processPayment` sends `percentageReferral=10` to the
  referral wallet (50% USDT + 50% SLEARN minted via `mintAndReserve`). Funded by
  reducing pdJ (55% → 50%) per REQ #128 — not from scholarships.
- Off-chain (atomic attribution): the backend reads the `ReferralReward` event
  and pays the referrer right after the purchase, in the same USDT/SLEARN
  amounts received. The reward value varies by the currency used to pay (SLEARN
  has a 10% discount).

**Form 2 — missional course scholarships (only "Una relación con Jesús").**
When a referred user earns a crossword scholarship in a missional course, the
referrer earns 10% of the scholarship value.
- The student keeps 100% of their scholarship (no deduction from the beca).
- The referrer is paid from the referral wallet. If it is empty, no reward.

**Landing page + fund visibility (done):**

- Landing page `/referrals` (EN/ES) explains both forms and shows the available
