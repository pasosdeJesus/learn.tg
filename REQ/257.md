## 1. Objective

A bilingual course that teaches how to run a small, honest business as an act of
**stewardship**: the business is not only a way to earn a living, it is a place where
the owner serves people, keeps their word and administers what was entrusted to them.
The course is practical (each guide ends with an exercise in **Diligent Records**,
R-#258) and missional (it prepares pastors and church members to sustain their families
and their ministries without depending forever on donations).

**Working title:** *Small Business Development: Stewardship & Purpose* (ES: *Desarrollo
de Pequeños Negocios: Mayordomía y Propósito*).

## 2. Framing (and what it is not)

- **Stewardship**: "The plans of the diligent lead to abundance" (Proverbs 21:5) —
  diligence, honesty, order in the accounts; what we have is administered, not owned
  (Psalm 24:1).
- **Purpose**: a business exists to serve people well and to create value, not only to
  extract it; profit is a means, not the end (Micah 6:8, Luke 16:10-11).
- **Not**: a get-rich scheme, a finance course, or a guarantee of success. No promises
  of profit; the material must warn about risk, debt and the temptation to cut corners.
- **Honest about money**: prices, interest and scams are discussed plainly; the course
  keeps the platform's rule of **clean money** (PRINCIPLES.md §7).

## 3. Course shape

- **Language**: English and Spanish, kept in sync (`resources/en/...`,
  `resources/es/...`).
- **Guide count**: 8 guides, each one a self-contained module per the **Five Pillars**
  of `doc/guide-writing.md` (400-600 words, one objective, an action step, 6-8
  comprehension questions of which the platform shows at most 5, and a crossword).
- **Proposed outline** (to review with the operator before writing):
  1. **Stewardship: what you have was entrusted to you** — purpose before profit.
  2. **Serving your customer** — finding a real need; honesty as the best marketing.
  3. **Counting what matters** — income, expenses, and why a written record is not
     bureaucracy but protection (Diligent Records exercise).
  4. **Pricing with justice** — cost, margin, what a fair price is, when to raise it.
  5. **Saving and reinvesting** — separating the household from the business, saving in
     stable money instead of losing value to inflation (links to the stable-sl course).
  6. **Debt: when it helps and when it enslaves** — interest, guarantees, saying no.
  7. **A team you can trust** — family and employees, paying what is right, delegating.
  8. **Generosity and community** — giving, the church, the cluster and the country
     (links to the GD/ClusterFunds material).
- Every guide links to the next and to the tool; guides 3-8 end with a Diligent Records
  exercise the learner can do offline (R-#256) and repeats the previous exercise.

## 4. Delivery steps (from `doc/how-to-create-a-course.md`)

1. **Scaffold**: `./scripts/create-course.sh "<Name>" en "<url-prefix>" 8 "<USDT>" "<SLEARN>" ["<icon>"]`,
   run a second time with `es` for the Spanish version.
2. **DB**: review and run the generated migration (`bin/m db:migrate`); `nombrecorto`
   controls the guide order; `sufijoRuta` must match the file name.
3. **Content**: write the 8 guides in both languages; each with 6+ questions and a
   crossword; absolute cross-references between guides.
4. **Vault**: create the course vault on `LearnTGVaultsV5`
   (`createVault(courseId, amountPerGuideUSDT, amountPerGuideSLEARN)`) and fund it, or
   register it as a missional course if the team decides it should be paid by donations.
5. **Credential (SBT)**: `bin/m credentials:register-type --site learn.tg --type
   course_completion --course-id <id> --icon <512x512 svg>` (+ `--premium` if it is
   paid), then `credentials:sync-cache`.
6. **Catalog/transparency**: the course must appear in the catalog (en/es) with its
   image, and its vault in the transparency page.

## 5. Decisions to confirm (operator)

1. **Price model**: premium with the HDI formula (recommended: the platform already has
   the machinery and the SLEARN the learner earns pays for it — the GD course is the
   precedent) or free with a scholarship vault funded by donations (missional). One of
   the two must be chosen before step 4; it changes the vault, the SBT (`--premium`) and
   the copy.
2. **Who may read it**: everybody, or GD pastors first (the `gdcluster` pattern).
3. **Guides 3-8 depend on Diligent Records** being usable (R-#258); if the tool slips,
   the course can ship with a paper/pencil version of the exercises and add the tool
   later.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The course exists in the catalog in English and Spanish, with its image, title,
      subtitle and scholarship information.
- [ ] The 8 guides exist in `resources/{en,es}/<prefix>/` with 6+ questions each
      (format `___ ... (answer)`), and the parser shows 3-5 in each crossword.
- [ ] Every guide follows the Five Pillars (400-600 words, one objective, an action
      step, joyful tone) and is bilingual and in sync.
- [ ] The vault is created (V5) and funded (or registered missional), with the
      scholarship formula documented in the course page.
- [ ] The course credential (SBT) is registered and mints at 100% completion.
- [ ] The Diligent Records exercises are described in the guides and match the tool's
      real screens (R-#258).
- [ ] The course is readable offline once downloaded (R-#256).
- [ ] `make type`, the unit suite and the course-related E2E stay green.

## 7. Out of scope

- Formal certification, accreditation or a business plan competition.
- Advanced accounting (payroll, taxes, audit), investments or lending products.
- The tool itself (R-#258) and its data model.
- Translation to languages other than English and Spanish (a later translation pass).
