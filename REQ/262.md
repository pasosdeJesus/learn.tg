## 1. Objective

Give a learner a **guided, interactive tool** to write their business plan as
they advance through the 16 lessons of *Small Business Development: Stewardship
& Purpose* (R-#257). Each lesson feeds a section of the plan; by lesson 15, the
learner has a complete draft they can generate as a PDF.

The tool is the practice artifact of the course's business plan lessons. It
consumes real financial data from **Diligent Records** (R-#258) for the financial
section, and works with **Budget Creator** (R-#263) for the budget section.

## 2. Data model (Kysely migration, `db/migrations/`)

### 2.1 `diligent_business_plan` — the plan document

- `id` (PK)
- `usuario_id` (FK to `usuario`)
- `plan_id` (FK to `diligent_plan` in R-#258) — links to the accounting plan
- `version` (int)
- `sections` (JSONB) — one object per lesson:
  - `mission` (text)
  - `skills` (text)
  - `values` (text)
  - `research` (JSONB)
  - `swot` (JSONB: strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats)
  - `customers` (JSONB)
  - `market` (JSONB)
  - `location` (text)
  - `goals` (JSONB)
  - `pricing` (JSONB)
  - `financials` (JSONB) — computed from Diligent Records
  - `budget` (JSONB) — computed from Budget Creator
  - `marketing` (JSONB)
  - `focus` (text)
  - `integrity` (text)
- `generated_at` (timestamp)
- `last_export_at` (timestamp)
- timestamps

### 2.2 `diligent_business_plan_export` — export history

- `id`, `usuario_id`, `plan_id`, `format` (`pdf` | `csv`), `created_at`

## 3. Design

1. **Offline first.** The tool reads its plan from IndexedDB and writes there
   first; a queue replays when the connection returns, with `client_id` for
   idempotency.

2. **Server routes** (all `authenticateUser` + `doc/api-security.md`):
   - `GET/POST /api/diligent/business-plan`
   - `GET /api/diligent/business-plan/pdf`
   - `POST /api/diligent/business-plan/export`

3. **Integration with Diligent Records (R-#258).** The `financials` section is
   computed from `GET /api/diligent/summary` (real income, expenses, profit)
   and `GET /api/diligent/entries` (detail). The tool does not duplicate data;
   it reads it.

4. **Integration with Budget Creator (R-#263).** The `budget` section is
   computed from the budget of the linked `plan_id`. The tool does not duplicate
   data; it reads it.

5. **Each lesson feeds a section.** When the learner completes a lesson in
   R-#257, the tool prompts them to fill the corresponding section. The section
   is saved and the plan draft updates.

6. **PDF generation.** The plan can be exported as a PDF with:
   - Cover (business name, owner, date)
   - Each section in order
   - Financial tables (from Diligent Records)
   - Budget tables (from Budget Creator)
   - Notes and reflections

7. **Privacy.** The plan is private to the user. No third-party names leave the
   server. Notes are free text but the UI discourages personal names.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An authenticated user can create a business plan and fill its sections as
      they advance through the course.
- [ ] The `financials` section is computed from Diligent Records data (no
      duplication).
- [ ] The `budget` section is computed from Budget Creator data (no
      duplication).
- [ ] The plan can be exported as a PDF with all sections and tables.
- [ ] The tool works offline; on reconnect it is replayed exactly once.
- [ ] `GET /api/diligent/business-plan*` and `POST /api/diligent/business-plan*`
      authenticate; `bin/audit-api-auth.mjs` reports 0 failed.
- [ ] Unit tests for the sections, the PDF generation and the queue replay.
- [ ] `make type` and the full suite green.

## 5. Out of scope

- Formal certification or accreditation.
- Multi-user or shared plans.
- Integration with SLEARN/SBT (that is in another session).
- The other tools (R-#258, R-#263) themselves.
