## 1. Objective

Give a learner a **guided, interactive tool** to write their business plan as
they advance through the lessons of *Small Business Development: Stewardship &
Purpose* (R-#257). Each lesson feeds a section of the plan; by lesson 15, the
learner has a complete draft they can generate as a PDF.

The tool is the practice artifact of the course's business plan lessons. It
**consumes** real financial data from **Diligent Records light** (R-#258) for
the financial section. It works alongside **Budget Creator** (R-#263), which
consumes the same R-#258 model for the budget section. The plan and the budget
are **separate documents**; the plan references the budget, it does not embed it.

**No SLEARN, SBT or stable-sl in this tool.** Those belong to other REQ
(R-#192, R-#278, stable-sl) and are out of scope here.

## 2. What exists today

Nothing. The tool does not exist yet. The course of R-#257 is being written
and expects this tool for lesson 15.

## 3. Data model

**This REQ does not duplicate the R-#258 domain model.** The tables
`diligent_plan`, `diligent_account`, `diligent_budget`, `diligent_budget_line`,
`diligent_transaction`, `diligent_transaction_entry`, `diligent_export` and
`diligent_upload` live in R-#258 §3. This REQ **adds one table** for its own
document (the plan text), and **consumes** the R-#258 tables via API.

### 3.1 `diligent_business_plan` — the plan document (this REQ's own table)

- `id` (PK)
- `usuario_id` (FK to `usuario`)
- `diligent_plan_id` (FK to `diligent_plan` in R-#258) — links the document to
  the accounting plan
- `version` (int) — incremented on each save
- `sections` (JSONB) — one object per lesson, keyed by lesson id:
  - `mission` (text)
  - `skills` (text)
  - `values` (text)
  - `research` (JSONB)
  - `swot` (JSONB: strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats)
  - `customers` (JSONB)
  - `market` (JSONB)
  - `location` (text)
  - `goals` (JSONB)
  - `pricing` (JSONB)
  - `financials` (JSONB) — **computed on read** from R-#258 (not stored)
  - `budget` (JSONB) — **computed on read** from R-#258 (not stored)
  - `marketing` (JSONB)
  - `focus` (text)
  - `integrity` (text)
- `generated_at` (timestamp)
- `last_export_at` (timestamp)
- timestamps

**The `financials` and `budget` sections are not stored.** They are computed
from the R-#258 API when the plan is read or exported, so they never drift from
the real figures.

### 3.2 `diligent_business_plan_export` — export history (this REQ's own table)

- `id` (PK)
- `usuario_id` (FK to `usuario`)
- `business_plan_id` (FK to `diligent_business_plan`)
- `format` (`pdf` | `json`)
- `created_at`

**R-#258's `diligent_export` is not reused**: it records exports of the
accounting domain (entries, budget, plan of accounts), not of the business plan
document. Two different concerns, two tables.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Offline first

The tool reads its plan from IndexedDB and writes there first; a queue replays
when the connection returns, with `client_id` (UUID) for idempotency. The tool
reuses R-#258's offline plumbing (`lib/offline-db.ts`, `lib/offline-queue-db.ts`,
`useOfflineQueue`).

### 4.2 Server routes (all `authenticateUser` + `doc/api-security.md`)

This REQ adds **its own routes** for the plan document. It **does not add** new
`diligent/plans`, `diligent/accounts`, `diligent/budgets` or `diligent/entries`
routes; those live in R-#258 and this tool **consumes** them.

| Route | Methods | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `/api/diligent/business-plan` | GET, POST | Read/save the plan document |
| `/api/diligent/business-plan/[id]` | GET, PATCH, DELETE | Plan detail/edit |
| `/api/diligent/business-plan/[id]/pdf` | GET | Generate the PDF |
| `/api/diligent/business-plan/[id]/export` | POST | Record an export in `diligent_business_plan_export` |

### 4.3 Integration with Diligent Records (R-#258) — consume, do not duplicate

The `financials` section is computed from **R-#258's** endpoints:

- `GET /api/diligent/summary` (aggregates: income, expenses, balance, by
  currency and period) — for the financial summary tables.
- `GET /api/diligent/entries` (detail) — for the appendix of transactions, if
  the learner wants to include it.

The `budget` section is computed from **R-#258's** `GET /api/diligent/budget-vs-real`
(per account and period).

**The tool does not duplicate this data.** It reads it at plan read time and at
PDF generation time. If R-#258 has no data yet (the learner has not recorded
entries), the tool shows the planned figures from its own sections and a
notice: "Once you start recording in Diligent Records, the real figures will
appear here."

The API contract is **versioned** (`{ version: 1, data: ... }`, R-#258 §4.10).
This tool must handle `version: 1` and treat an empty response as "no data yet"
(empty array, not error).

### 4.4 Integration with Budget Creator (R-#263) — sibling, not dependency

Budget Creator is a **sibling tool**, not a dependency. Both consume R-#258.
The plan's `budget` section is computed from R-#258 directly (via
`/api/diligent/budget-vs-real`), not from R-#263. If R-#263 adds a template or
a view, that is R-#263's concern; the plan does not depend on it.

**No circular dependency:** R-#262 does not import R-#263's code, tables or
routes. Both depend on R-#258.

### 4.5 Each lesson feeds a section

When the learner completes a lesson in R-#257, the tool prompts them to fill the
corresponding section (`sections[lesson_id]`). The section is saved and the
plan draft updates.

### 4.6 PDF generation

The plan can be exported as a PDF with:

- Cover (business name, owner, date)
- Each section in order (including `financials` and `budget`, computed on read)
- Financial tables (from R-#258 `summary`)
- Budget tables (from R-#258 `budget-vs-real`)
- Notes and reflections

The PDF is generated **server-side** and recorded in
`diligent_business_plan_export`.

### 4.7 Privacy

The plan is private to the user. No third-party names leave the server. Notes
are free text but the UI discourages personal names ("cliente A", not the
person's name). The route list is audited with `bin/audit-api-auth.mjs`.

### 4.8 Simple mode

The plan tool shows all sections, but the learner can hide the sections they
have not reached yet (`sections` with no content are collapsed). The tool
follows the course: each lesson reveals its section. There is no separate
"simple mode" toggle; the course progression is the mode.

### 4.9 Money in the learner's terms

Amounts in the `financials` and `budget` sections are shown as they come from
R-#258 (two decimals, per currency: Leones and USD side by side, no
conversion). The tool does not convert or move money.

### 4.10 No SLEARN, SBT or stable-sl

The tool does not touch SLEARN, SBTs or stable-sl. Those are out of scope
(R-#192, R-#278, stable-sl).

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An authenticated user can create a business plan and fill its sections as
      they advance through the course.
- [ ] The `financials` section is computed **on read** from R-#258's
      `/api/diligent/summary` (no duplication, no stored totals).
- [ ] The `budget` section is computed **on read** from R-#258's
      `/api/diligent/budget-vs-real` (no duplication, no stored totals).
- [ ] The plan can be exported as a PDF with all sections and the computed
      tables.
- [ ] The tool works offline; on reconnect it is replayed exactly once
      (`client_id`).
- [ ] The tool handles the "no Diligent Records data yet" case (empty response,
      not error) and shows the notice.
- [ ] `GET /api/diligent/business-plan*` and `POST /api/diligent/business-plan*`
      authenticate; `bin/audit-api-auth.mjs` reports **0 failed**.
- [ ] Unit tests for the sections, the computed `financials`/`budget` (with
      mocked R-#258 responses), the PDF generation and the queue replay.
- [ ] `make type` and the full suite green.

## 6. Out of scope

- Formal certification or accreditation.
- Multi-user or shared plans.
- **SLEARN, SBT and stable-sl integration** (out of scope for this REQ; see
  R-#192, R-#278, stable-sl).
- The other tools (R-#258, R-#263) themselves: this REQ consumes R-#258's model
  and does not depend on R-#263.
- The detailed mapping between each course lesson and each section (that is
  R-#257's concern).
- The full edition of Diligent Records (separate application, another REQ).
