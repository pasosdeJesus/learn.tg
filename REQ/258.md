## 1. Objective

Give a learner a **professional, offline-first book of accounts** for a small
business, good enough to teach order and honesty with money, and simple enough
to be used on a cheap phone in an area with poor connectivity. Every figure the
learner records must be theirs (private, authenticated) and must survive a lost
connection.

The tool is the practice artifact of *Small Business Development: Stewardship &
Purpose* (R-#257): each lesson ends with an exercise done in it. It also feeds
the **Business Plan Creator** (R-#262) and the **Budget Creator** (R-#263) with
real financial data, and is used by the operator for missionary travel
accounting with reports to donors.

## 3. Data model (Kysely migration, `db/migrations/`)

### 3.1 `diligent_plan` — the business plan or trip

- `id` (PK)
- `usuario_id` (FK to `usuario`)
- `nombre` (varchar 255) — "Taller de costura Gideon", "Viaje misionero SL Sep 2025"
- `tipo` (`business` | `trip` | `ministry` | `personal`)
- `fecha_inicio` (date)
- `fecha_fin` (date, nullable)
- `pais` (char 2, ISO)
- `proposito` (text)
- `mision` (text)
- `metas` (JSONB) — expected sales, growth, etc.
- `proyecciones` (JSONB) — estimated income/expenses
- `moneda_principal` (`SLE` | `USD`)
- `estado` (`planificado` | `en_curso` | `cerrado`)
- `metadata` (JSONB)
- timestamps

### 3.2 `diligent_account` — chart of accounts (per plan)

- `id` (PK)
- `plan_id` (FK to `diligent_plan`)
- `usuario_id` (FK to `usuario`, redundant for queries)
- `codigo` (varchar 100) — "expense:merchandise", "revenue:sales", etc.
- `nombre` (varchar 255) — "Mercadería", "Ventas", etc.
- `tipo` (`asset` | `liability` | `equity` | `revenue` | `expense`)
- `moneda` (`SLE` | `USD`)
- `es_personalizada` (boolean)
- `is_active` (boolean)
- timestamps
- UNIQUE(plan_id, codigo, moneda)

Seeded on first use with a minimal chart (cash, sales, purchases, transport,
rent, personal withdrawal, savings, and travel categories when `tipo = 'trip'`).

### 3.3 `diligent_budget` — budget container

- `id` (PK)
- `plan_id` (FK to `diligent_plan`)
- `nombre` (varchar 255) — "Presupuesto anual 2026", "Presupuesto mensual oct"
- `periodo` (`mensual` | `trimestral` | `anual` | `unico`)
- `fecha_inicio` (date)
- `fecha_fin` (date)
- `moneda` (`SLE` | `USD`)
- timestamps

### 3.4 `diligent_budget_line` — budget line per account

- `id` (PK)
- `budget_id` (FK to `diligent_budget`)
- `account_id` (FK to `diligent_account`)
- `monto_presupuestado` (numeric 14,2)
- `moneda` (`SLE` | `USD`)
- UNIQUE(budget_id, account_id)

### 3.5 `diligent_transaction` — one operation

- `id` (PK)
- `usuario_id` (FK to `usuario`)
- `transaction_id` (UUID, unique) — idempotency key
- `plan_id` (FK to `diligent_plan`)
- `budget_id` (FK to `diligent_budget`, nullable)
- `fecha` (date)
- `descripcion` (varchar 255)
- `tipo` (`income` | `expense` | `transfer` | `loan` | `repayment`)
- `source` (`online` | `offline`)
- `receipt_url` (text, nullable) — photo of receipt
- `metadata` (JSONB)
- timestamps

### 3.6 `diligent_transaction_entry` — lines of the operation

- `id` (PK)
- `transaction_id` (FK to `diligent_transaction`)
- `account_id` (FK to `diligent_account`)
- `amount` (numeric 14,2, **signed**: + adds to account, − subtracts)
- `currency` (`SLE` | `USD`)
- timestamps

### 3.7 `diligent_export` — export history

- `id`, `usuario_id`, `plan_id` (nullable), `from`, `to`, `format` (`csv` | `pdf`),
  `created_at`

### 3.8 Derived, never stored

Daily/weekly/monthly summaries, P&L/balance reports, and budget vs. real
comparisons are **computed** from `diligent_transaction` and
`diligent_transaction_entry`. The cache-trigger policy of ARCHITECTURE.md does
not apply (no aggregate table yet; if one is added it must be trigger-fed).

## 4. Design

1. **Offline first.** The tool reads its accounts from IndexedDB (`diligent`
   store in `learn-tg-offline`) and writes entries there first; a queue (same
   shape as `offline-queue-db`, or the existing one) replays them when the
   connection returns, with `transaction_id` (UUID) for idempotency. The UI
   never blocks on the network.

2. **Server routes** (all `authenticateUser` + `doc/api-security.md`):
   - `GET/POST /api/diligent/plans`
   - `GET/POST /api/diligent/accounts`
   - `GET/POST /api/diligent/budgets`
   - `GET/POST /api/diligent/entries` (paged, `from`/`to`, `plan_id`, `budget_id`)
   - `GET /api/diligent/summary`
   - `GET /api/diligent/budget-vs-real`
   - `GET /api/diligent/export.csv`
   - `POST` accepts a batch of queued entries and answers per item (accepted /
     duplicate / rejected with reason).

3. **Privacy.** Amounts are the user's business: no third-party `nombre` ever
   leaves the server (the API rule), no PII in logs, and the route list is
   audited with `bin/audit-api-auth.mjs`. Notes are free text but the UI must
   discourage names ("cliente A", not the person's name).

4. **Money in the learner's terms.** Amounts are entered as text with two
   decimals and stored as `numeric(14,2)`; the currency is per account (Leones
   and USDT side by side, no conversion in the tool: converting is the job of
   `stable-sl`). No crypto amounts are moved by the tool: it only records them.

5. **Reports, kept teachable.** Daily, weekly and monthly summaries by account;
   a simple P&L and a cash balance; and a **budget vs. real** view per account
   and period. No jargon: "what came in", "what went out", "what is left", and
   one line per figure explaining what it means (this is course material).

6. **Trip mode.** When `diligent_plan.tipo = 'trip'`, the tool groups
   transactions by plan, uses travel-specific accounts (`expense:travel:lodging`,
   `expense:travel:food`, `expense:travel:transport`, `revenue:donations:*`,
   `expense:ministry:*`), and generates a **trip report** in PDF/CSV for donors
   with: date, account, description, amount, currency, receipt photo (optional).

7. **Export.** CSV of the entries of a period (or of a trip), downloadable, so a
   learner can hand it to their church or a microfinance officer. The export is
   generated server-side and the history is recorded in `diligent_export`.

8. **The tool is the course's practice.** Each exercise of R-#257 maps to a
   concrete action in the tool (record a sale, record a purchase, close the
   week, look at the balance, compare budget vs. real). The "demo" placeholders
   are removed and the page keeps: the install prompt, the session/wallet state
   and the offline indicator.

9. **Its own install and cache.** `public/manifest.json` and the
   `/[lang]/diligent-records*` cache entry stay as they are; the page must work
   offline for the entries already opened (its own `NetworkFirst` page entry
   plus the IndexedDB store).

10. **Integration with Business Plan (R-#262) and Budget (R-#263) via API.**
    - Diligent Records exposes `GET /api/diligent/summary` (aggregates) and
      `GET /api/diligent/entries` (detail) for R-#262 and R-#263 to consume.
    - Business Plan Creator uses the real totals for the financial section of
      the plan.
    - Budget Creator uses real income/expenses to compare against the planned
      budget.
    - **Diligent Records does not know or depend on R-#262 or R-#263**; it only
      exposes data. The integration is unidirectional (they consume).

11. **Subscription (out of scope for this first version).** The data model
    includes `diligent_plan` with room for `plan` (`free` | `small` | `medium` |
    `large`) and `billing_cycle`. The subscription logic according to business
    size will be implemented in a later requirement (R-#264), not in this one.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An authenticated user can create plans, accounts and budgets; record
      entries and see daily/weekly/monthly summaries; everything is private to
      that user (another wallet cannot read it, including with a valid session
      of its own).
- [ ] Recording an entry offline works; on reconnect it is replayed exactly once
      (`transaction_id`), and the summary reflects it.
- [ ] The summary and the P&L/balance are computed from the entries (no stored
      totals to drift) and are explained in plain language for the course.
- [ ] The **budget vs. real** view works per account and period.
- [ ] Export to CSV of a period works and is recorded in `diligent_export`.
- [ ] **Trip mode:** a user can create a trip, assign entries to it, and export
      a trip report in CSV/PDF with date, account, description, amount,
      currency.
- [ ] The operator (vtamara) can keep the accounting of a missionary trip and
      generate a donor report from the tool.
- [ ] The page still installs as an app and shows the offline indicator; its
      manifest and cache entry are unchanged.
- [ ] `GET /api/diligent/*` and `POST /api/diligent/*` authenticate;
      `node bin/audit-api-auth.mjs` reports **0 failed**.
- [ ] Unit tests for the store, the summary maths, the budget-vs-real
      calculation and the queue replay; E2E: record an entry offline, reconnect,
      see it in the summary.
- [ ] `make type` and the full suite green.

## 6. Out of scope

- Payroll, taxes, invoicing with legal validity, multi-company or accountant
  roles.
- Double-entry enforcement beyond what the ledger already provides,
  bank reconciliation, imports from bank statements.
- Moving or converting real money (that is `stable-sl` and the payment flows).
- A public/shared view of the accounts (a later "show it to my church" feature,
  if the course asks for it).
- SLEARN/SBT integration (that responsibility is in another session).
- Subscription logic (R-#264, later).
- The Business Plan Creator (R-#262) and Budget Creator (R-#263) themselves:
  Diligent Records only exposes data for them.
