## 1. Objective

Give a learner a **professional, offline-first book of accounts** for a small
business, good enough to teach order and honesty with money, and simple enough
to be used on a cheap phone in an area with poor connectivity. Every figure the
learner records must be theirs (private, authenticated) and must survive a lost
connection.

The tool is the practice artifact of *Small Business Development: Stewardship &
Purpose* (R-#257): the course has three tools, in this order — **Business Plan
Creator** (R-#262) → **Budget Creator** (R-#263) → **Diligent Records**
(this REQ). Each lesson of the course ends with an exercise done in the
corresponding tool; Diligent Records is the last one, where the learner keeps
the actual accounts of the business they planned and budgeted.

It also feeds the Business Plan Creator and the Budget Creator with real
financial data (they consume; Diligent Records only exposes).

### 1.1 Two editions

| Edition | Where | Cost | Storage | Money volume | IndexedDB |
|---------|-------|------|---------|--------------|-----------|
| **Light** (this REQ) | Inside learn.tg (`/[lang]/diligent-records`) | Included with the business course (R-#257); no monthly fee | **500 MB per user** in `uploads/usuario/<id>/` | No cap in the light edition (see §4.11) | learn.tg's `learn-tg-offline` (store `diligent`) |
| **Full** (another REQ) | Separate application, own domain | **Monthly fee** by storage and money volume | Larger, paid tiers | Paid tiers | Its **own** IndexedDB |

The light edition is the one this REQ describes. The full edition is a
**separate application** with its own domain, its own IndexedDB, and **its own
data model**. It may not use the same tables or the same `diligent_*` schema.
The light edition can **export all data of one business as JSON** (§4.9), and
the full edition can **import that JSON** to onboard a business that outgrew
the light limits, mapping it to its own schema.

The **complete data model of the light edition** lives in this REQ (§3).
R-#262 and R-#263 **consume it** (they call `/api/diligent/*`); they do not
duplicate tables, routes or histories.

**No SLEARN, SBT or stable-sl in the light edition.** Those belong to other
REQ (R-#192, R-#278, stable-sl) and are out of scope here.

## 3. Data model (Kysely migration, `db/migrations/`)

**This is the data model of the light edition.** The full edition may have a
different model. R-#262 and R-#263 consume the light edition's API; they do not
depend on the full edition's model.

R-#262 and R-#263 **may add their own tables** (the plan document, the budget
template, their export history), but they **do not duplicate** the domain tables
listed in §3.1–§3.6.

### 3.1 `diligent_plan` — the business plan

- `id` (PK)
- `usuario_id` (FK to `usuario`)
- `nombre` (varchar 255)
- `tipo` (`business` | `ministry` | `personal`) — default `business`
- `fecha_inicio` (date)
- `fecha_fin` (date, nullable)
- `pais` (char 2, ISO)
- `proposito` (text)
- `mision` (text)
- `metas` (JSONB)
- `proyecciones` (JSONB)
- `moneda_principal` (`SLE` | `USD`)
- `estado` (`planificado` | `en_curso` | `cerrado`)
- `metadata` (JSONB) — includes `simple_mode` (boolean, default `true`, §4.5)
- timestamps

### 3.2 `diligent_account` — chart of accounts (per plan)

- `id` (PK)
- `plan_id` (FK to `diligent_plan`)
- `usuario_id` (FK to `usuario`, redundant for queries)
- `codigo` (varchar 100) — "expense:merchandise", "revenue:sales", etc.
- `nombre` (varchar 255)
- `tipo` (`asset` | `liability` | `equity` | `revenue` | `expense`)
- `moneda` (`SLE` | `USD`)
- `es_personalizada` (boolean)
- `is_active` (boolean)
- timestamps
- UNIQUE(plan_id, codigo, moneda)

Seeded on first use with a minimal chart (cash, sales, purchases, transport,
rent, personal withdrawal, savings). The learner can add, rename, or deactivate
accounts. Travel categories are just one possible configuration, not a special
mode.

### 3.3 `diligent_budget` — budget container

- `id` (PK)
- `plan_id` (FK to `diligent_plan`)
- `nombre` (varchar 255)
- `periodo` (`mensual` | `trimestral` | `anual` | `unico`)
- `fecha_inicio` (date)
- `fecha_fin` (date)
- `moneda` (`SLE` | `USD`)
- timestamps

### 3.4 `diligent_budget_line` — budget line per account

- `id` (PK)
- `budget_id` (FK to `diligent_budget`)
- `account_id` (FK to `diligent_account`)
- `monto_presupuestado` (numeric 14,2)
- `moneda` (`SLE` | `USD`)
- UNIQUE(budget_id, account_id)

### 3.5 `diligent_transaction` — one operation

- `id` (PK)
- `usuario_id` (FK to `usuario`)
- `transaction_id` (UUID, unique) — idempotency key
- `plan_id` (FK to `diligent_plan`)
- `budget_id` (FK to `diligent_budget`, nullable)
- `fecha` (date)
- `descripcion` (varchar 255)
- `tipo` (`income` | `expense` | `transfer` | `loan` | `repayment`)
- `source` (`online` | `offline`)
- `receipt_url` (text, nullable) — stored in `uploads/usuario/<usuario_id>/`
- `metadata` (JSONB)
- timestamps

### 3.6 `diligent_transaction_entry` — lines of the operation

- `id` (PK)
- `transaction_id` (FK to `diligent_transaction`)
- `account_id` (FK to `diligent_account`)
- `amount` (numeric 14,2, **signed**: + adds to account, − subtracts)
- `currency` (`SLE` | `USD`)
- timestamps

### 3.7 `diligent_export` — export history (light edition only)

- `id` (PK)
- `usuario_id` (FK to `usuario`)
- `entity` (`entries` | `budget` | `plan`)
- `entity_id` (int, nullable)
- `format` (`csv` | `json` | `pdf`)
- `from` (date, nullable)
- `to` (date, nullable)
- `created_at`

**This table is only for the light edition.** R-#262 and R-#263, if they need
to record their own exports, create their own tables.

### 3.8 `diligent_upload` — file quota tracking (light edition only)

- `id` (PK)
- `usuario_id` (FK to `usuario`)
- `file_path` (varchar 255) — `uploads/usuario/<id>/...`
- `file_size` (bigint)
- `created_at`

Records the files uploaded for receipts. The quota is computed from this table,
not from `stat()` on every request. Populated on upload, cleaned on delete.

### 3.9 Derived, never stored

Daily/weekly/monthly summaries, P&L/balance reports, and budget vs. real
comparisons are **computed** from `diligent_transaction` and
`diligent_transaction_entry`. No aggregate table.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Offline first

The tool reads its data from IndexedDB (`diligent` store in
`learn-tg-offline`) and writes there first; a queue replays when the connection
returns, with `transaction_id` (UUID) for idempotency. The UI never blocks on
the network.

The route **reuses learn.tg's Service Worker**. No additional SW is registered.
The `runtimeCaching` entry for `/[lang]/diligent-records*` ensures the page
shell is available offline; the data lives in IndexedDB.

### 4.2 Server routes (all `authenticateUser` + `doc/api-security.md`)

**This is the complete set of routes for the light edition.** R-#262 and R-#263
do not add new `diligent_*` routes; they consume these.

| Route | Methods | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `/api/diligent/plans` | GET, POST | List/create plans |
| `/api/diligent/plans/[id]` | GET, PATCH, DELETE | Plan detail/edit |
| `/api/diligent/accounts` | GET, POST | List/create accounts |
| `/api/diligent/accounts/[id]` | PATCH, DELETE | Edit/deactivate account |
| `/api/diligent/budgets` | GET, POST | List/create budgets |
| `/api/diligent/budgets/[id]` | GET, PATCH, DELETE | Budget detail/edit |
| `/api/diligent/entries` | GET, POST | List/create entries (paged, `from`/`to`, `plan_id`, `budget_id`) |
| `/api/diligent/entries/[id]` | GET, PATCH, DELETE | Entry detail/edit |
| `/api/diligent/summary` | GET | Aggregates (income, expenses, balance, by period) |
| `/api/diligent/budget-vs-real` | GET | Budget vs. real per account |
| `/api/diligent/export` | POST | Create export (csv/json/pdf) |
| `/api/diligent/export/[id]` | GET | Download an export |
| `/api/diligent/import` | POST | Import JSON into the light edition (round-trip testing) |

`POST /api/diligent/entries` accepts a batch of queued entries and answers per
item (`accepted` / `duplicate` / `rejected` with reason). The server validates
that each `diligent_transaction` has at least two entries and that the sum of
`amount` per currency is zero; otherwise it rejects with a clear message.

**Idempotency:** if a `transaction_id` already exists, the server answers
`duplicate` and **does not** modify the existing transaction. To edit, the
client creates a new `transaction_id` (or an adjustment transaction).

### 4.3 Privacy

Amounts are the user's business: no third-party `nombre` ever leaves the
server, no PII in logs, and the route list is audited with
`bin/audit-api-auth.mjs`. Notes are free text but the UI must discourage names
("cliente A", not the person's name).

### 4.4 Money in the learner's terms

Amounts are entered as text with two decimals and stored as `numeric(14,2)`;
the currency is per account (Leones and USD side by side, no conversion in the
tool). The tool only records amounts; it does not move money.

### 4.5 Simple mode by default

The UI defaults to a simplified view: record income, record expense, see what
came in and what went out. The double-entry accounts exist in the database but
are hidden from the learner until they complete the relevant lesson of R-#257.
The chart of accounts is seeded with 5–6 basic accounts, not a full accounting
plan.

When the learner completes the lesson that introduces double entry, the UI
shows a notice ("now you can see the detailed accounts") and a link to activate
the advanced view. The learner can return to the simple mode at any time.

The preference is stored in `diligent_plan.metadata.simple_mode` (boolean,
default `true`). The learner can toggle it back in the plan settings.

### 4.6 Configurable budget categories, no "trip mode"

The learner defines the budget categories (accounts) that fit their business.
A missionary trip is just one possible configuration, not a separate mode. The
reports are generic and work for any configuration.

### 4.7 Export

- **CSV** of the entries of a period, downloadable. The CSV includes a first
  line with the user, the date and a warning: "This file contains private
  business data. Do not share it with unauthorized people."
- **JSON** of all data of one business (plan, accounts, budgets, entries), for
  import into the full edition (§4.9).
- The export is generated server-side and recorded in `diligent_export`.

### 4.8 Receipts (`receipt_url`) and the 500 MB quota

`receipt_url` points to a file stored in **`uploads/usuario/<usuario_id>/`**
(not in the general `uploads/`). The light edition enforces a **500 MB
per-user quota** (total across all plans and receipts of that user).

| Threshold | Behavior |
|---|---|
| 80% of the quota | In-app warning: "You have used 80% of your storage. Consider exporting old receipts or moving to the full edition." |
| 100% of the quota | Upload refused with a message that links to the full edition (§1.1). The user can still record transactions without a receipt. |

The quota is computed from `diligent_upload` (§3.8), not from `stat()` on every
request. The UI warns: "Do not photograph documents with personal data (bank
details, ID numbers)."

### 4.9 JSON export/import (light → full)

The light edition can export **all data of one business** as a single JSON
file:

```json
{
  "version": 1,
  "exported_at": "2026-10-29T...",
  "plan": { ... },
  "accounts": [ ... ],
  "budgets": [ ... ],
  "budget_lines": [ ... ],
  "transactions": [ ... ],
  "entries": [ ... ]
}
```

The full edition (separate application) can **import** this JSON to onboard a
business that outgrew the light limits, mapping it to **its own data model**.
The import is idempotent on `transaction_id` and `account.codigo`.

`POST /api/diligent/import` accepts the JSON and creates the records in the
**light edition** (round-trip testing). The full edition has its **own** import
endpoint (another REQ); the light edition does not validate for the full
edition.

### 4.10 Integration with Business Plan (R-#262) and Budget (R-#263) via API

- Diligent Records exposes `GET /api/diligent/summary` and
  `GET /api/diligent/entries` for R-#262 and R-#263 to consume.
- **Diligent Records does not know or depend on R-#262 or R-#263**; it only
  exposes data. The integration is unidirectional (they consume).
- The endpoints return JSON with a versioned schema
  (`{ version: 1, data: ... }`). They authenticate with the same
  `authenticateUser` as the rest of the API. R-#262 and R-#263 must handle the
  case where the user has no Diligent Records data yet (empty array, not
  error).
- Before the learner records entries, R-#262 and R-#263 show the planned
  figures (from their own tables) and a notice: "Once you start recording in
  Diligent Records, the real figures will appear here."

**Contract of `/api/diligent/summary` (version 1):**

```json
{
  "version": 1,
  "data": {
    "plan_id": 123,
    "period": { "from": "2026-01-01", "to": "2026-12-31" },
    "by_currency": {
      "SLE": { "income": 0, "expenses": 0, "balance": 0 },
      "USD": { "income": 0, "expenses": 0, "balance": 0 }
    }
  }
}
```

**Contract of `/api/diligent/entries` (version 1):** paged list of transactions
with their entries, exposing `account_codigo` (not `account_id`) so consumers do
not need to resolve ids. Filters: `from`, `to`, `plan_id`, `budget_id`, `page`,
`limit`.

### 4.11 Subscription (out of scope for this first version)

The light edition has **no cap on money volume** and **no monthly fee**. The
full edition's pricing (by storage and money volume) is in another REQ
(R-#264 or a new one). The light model does **not** include subscription fields;
when a business moves to the full edition, the full edition manages its own
subscription.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

### Data and privacy
- [ ] An authenticated user can create plans, accounts and budgets; record
      entries and see daily/weekly/monthly summaries; everything is private to
      that user (another wallet cannot read it, including with a valid session
      of its own).
- [ ] The server validates that each transaction has at least two entries and
      that the sum of `amount` per currency is zero; otherwise it rejects.
- [ ] A `transaction_id` that already exists is answered as `duplicate` and the
      existing transaction is not modified.
- [ ] `receipt_url` files are stored in `uploads/usuario/<id>/`; the 500 MB
      per-user quota is enforced (80% warning, 100% refusal with a link to the
      full edition).
- [ ] The CSV export includes the privacy warning line.

### Offline and replay
- [ ] Recording an entry offline works; on reconnect it is replayed exactly once
      (`transaction_id`), and the summary reflects it.
- [ ] A rejected entry is shown in a **Rejected entries** panel (accessible from
      the plan view) with the reason and a button to correct or discard it; it
      is not retried automatically.

### Reports and budgets
- [ ] The summary and the P&L/balance are computed from the entries (no stored
      totals to drift) and are explained in plain language for the course.
- [ ] The **budget vs. real** view works per account and period.
- [ ] The learner can add, rename, or deactivate accounts to fit their business;
      travel categories are just one possible configuration.

### Export / import
- [ ] Export to CSV of a period works and is recorded in `diligent_export`.
- [ ] JSON export of one business works and includes plan, accounts, budgets,
      budget lines, transactions and entries.
- [ ] `POST /api/diligent/import` accepts the JSON and creates the records in
      the light edition (round-trip test).

### UI and performance
- [ ] The page still shows the offline indicator; its `runtimeCaching` entry is
      unchanged.
- [ ] **No separate PWA manifest**; the route lives within learn.tg's scope.
- [ ] The route loads in under 3 seconds on a 3G connection (measured with
      Lighthouse on Chrome DevTools with 3G throttling, documented in the REQ
      at closing). The route's JS bundle is under 200 KB gzipped (measured with
      `next build --analyze` or `source-map-explorer`).
- [ ] The tool is usable on a 5-inch screen with a 1 GB RAM phone: forms fit
      without horizontal scroll, the font size is at least 16 px, and the main
      actions (record income, record expense) are reachable in one tap from the
      plan view (manual test on a 5-inch device or DevTools emulation).

### API and tests
- [ ] `GET /api/diligent/*` and `POST /api/diligent/*` authenticate;
      `node bin/audit-api-auth.mjs` reports **0 failed**.
- [ ] Unit tests for the store, the summary maths, the budget-vs-real
      calculation, the queue replay, the double-entry validation, the
      idempotency and the JSON import.
- [ ] E2E: record an entry offline, reconnect, see it in the summary.
- [ ] `make type` and the full suite green.

## 6. Out of scope

- Payroll, taxes, invoicing with legal validity, multi-company or accountant
  roles.
- Double-entry enforcement beyond what the ledger already provides, bank
  reconciliation, imports from bank statements.
- Moving or converting real money.
- A public/shared view of the accounts.
- **SLEARN, SBT and stable-sl integration** (out of scope for this REQ; see
  R-#192, R-#278, stable-sl).
- Subscription logic (R-#264, later; the full edition has its own pricing).
- The Business Plan Creator (R-#262) and Budget Creator (R-#263) themselves:
  Diligent Records only exposes data for them. Their tables
  (`diligent_business_plan`, `diligent_budget_template`) live in their own REQ.
- **The full edition**: separate application (own domain, own IndexedDB, own
  data model, monthly fee by storage and money volume). This REQ describes the
  light edition and the JSON export/import bridge. The full edition is not
  constrained to the tables in §3.
- The linkage between course lessons and tool actions.
