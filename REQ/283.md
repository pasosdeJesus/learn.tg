# R-#283: Move and change en/gdcluster/pastors to en/pastor

Move the pastor landing out of the Global Disciples (GD) paths and turn it into a
neutral page about the welcome bonus, without announcing the GD course or its
"non-Zionist" condition publicly.

> **Operator request (2026-10-07):** instead of publicly announcing that the
> Global Disciples course is for non-Zionist pastors, present it **only** to
> verified non-Zionist pastors in the platform. `/en/gdcluster/pastor` must
> redirect to `/en/pastor` (and the analogous Spanish path). The content of
> `/en/pastor` is based on the previous landing but drops every mention of the GD
> course and of "non-Zionist". The 22 SLEARN bonus is for **every** pastor who
> verifies their data and their church's data while the churches fund has funds.
> Remove the "Country and cluster ranking" card that does not work well.

## 1. Problem

The old public landing (`GdPastoresLanding`, rendered at `/en/gdcluster/pastors`
and `/es/redgd/pastores`) announced, on a public URL, that the GD course is for
non-Zionist pastors, listed a "Country and cluster ranking" card that did not
work well, and leaned on the GD course as the pastor's goal. The bonus, in turn,
was gated by pilot country + `profilescore > 90`, which is stricter than the
operator's rule ("every pastor who verifies their data and their church").

## 2. What changes

### 2.1 The landing moves to `/{lang}/pastor`

- New route `apps/nextjs/app/[lang]/pastor/page.tsx` renders the new core
  component `apps/nextjs/components/PastorLanding.tsx`.
- The old paths keep working via a redirect:
  - `next.config.ts` `redirects()`: `/:lang(en|es)/:group(gdcluster|redgd)/:p(pastor|pastors|pastores)`
    → `/:lang/pastor` (`permanent: true`, 308).
  - The old page files (`app/[lang]/gdcluster/pastors/page.tsx`,
    `app/[lang]/redgd/pastores/page.tsx`) also `redirect()` to the same target,
    so a request that skips the config rule still lands correctly.
- `packages/gdcluster/src/components/ReferralsPage.tsx` (`pastorsHref`) links to
  `/{lang}/pastor`.

### 2.2 The new landing is neutral

`PastorLanding` is based on the old content but **removes**:

- every mention of the **Global Disciples** course (title, "Go to the GD course",
  "Why is the GD course important?", the GD subtitle/summary);
- every mention of the **non-Zionist** condition;
- the **"Country and cluster ranking"** card (`rankingTitle`, the country/cluster
  tables) that did not work well.

It keeps the churches-fund balance, the requirements checklist, the onboarding
path and the "New to wallets?" block. Requirements are: have a wallet, be a
pastor, provide the church registration, and be verified (role + church) by a
verifier.

### 2.3 Bonus rule: every verified pastor (fund-limited)

`packages/rewards/src/lib/pastor-bonus.ts`:

- `isEligiblePastor` is now `church_relationship === 'pastor' &&
  verified_church_relationship === 'pastor'` — no `ELIGIBLE_COUNTRIES`, no
  `MIN_SCORE_FOR_BONUS` (both exports removed).
- `awardPastorBonus` keeps the remaining gates: the church's lead pastor
  (`church.pastor_id`), `church.registration_verified`, a wallet, no duplicate
  bonus per church, and enough SLEARN in the churches fund. The redundant
  `verified_church_relationship` check was removed (already covered by
  `isEligiblePastor`).
- `apps/nextjs/app/api/admin/pastor-bonus/route.ts` drops the
  `usuario.pais_id in ELIGIBLE_COUNTRIES` filter.

### 2.4 The GD restriction is no longer announced publicly

- The GD course presentation stays hidden from ineligible users (`gdVisible` in
  `app/[lang]/[pathPrefix]/page.tsx` and the course list in
  `app/[lang]/page.tsx`): only a user whose declared religion matches the course
  (`religion_id = 2`) and who answered `no` to the Gaza question sees it.
- `apps/nextjs/lib/course-access-msg.ts`: the `gd_non_zionist` denial text was
  replaced by a neutral message ("This course is not available for your profile
  yet." / "Este curso aún no está disponible para tu perfil.") so the condition
  is not spelled out to users who do not meet it.
- The gate itself (`canPurchaseGDCourse`) is untouched.
- The GD course **subtitle** announced the condition on a public surface
  (`GET /api/course-catalog` is anonymous). Migration
  `20261008002842_neutral_gd_subtitle.ts` drops the "non-Zionist" sentence from
  the EN/ES subtitles; the course keeps its name and the gate.
