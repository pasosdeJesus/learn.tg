## 1. What works today (operator report, 2026-10-01, dev site)

Verified by hand and by the E2E `e2e/specs/wallet-panel-collectibles.spec.mjs`:

- create / import (recovery phrase or private key) / unlock (password, and the fingerprint/Face ID
  gesture), SIWE sign-in, disconnect and lock;
- the panel: the three balances (CELO/USDT/SLEARN), copy the address, receive with QR, send with
  destination/amount/balance validation and the fresh gesture before moving funds;
- **collectibles**: the panel's "Collectibles" tab lists the address's credentials read from
  Blockscout (4 shown for the `.env` wallet: Web3 & UBI, A relationship with Jesus, Donor,
  Connector). This is what the Web3 & UBI guide promises
  (`resources/en/web3-and-ubi/guide2.md`, "Step 4: Your collectibles").

So the in-app wallet is functional; the two items below remain.

## 2. To improve — visual design of the wallet panel

The operator finds the panel's visual appearance improvable. Starting points (not exhaustive):

- **The panel and the dialog are dense**: the tabs (balances/receive/send/collectibles), the copy
  button, the lock/close row and the notices compete for space on a 360-px phone; the panel opens
  from the header pill and should read as one clear surface.
- **Collectibles**: a 3-column grid of 64-px thumbnails with a truncated name; long course names
  (the same names the credentials truncate to 25 characters in their composed image, F-#195) get
  cut. Consider a larger, tappable item with the credential name and its date.
- **Dialog steps**: creation shows the password, the protection choice, the 12 words and the
  three-word confirmation in sequence; the wording and the emphasis of the primary action can be
  reviewed (the operator already reordered the protection step in R-#269).
- **Consistency**: the panel and the dialog are the only wallet UI in the app; align spacing, type
  scale and the use of the shadcn primitives across both.

A design pass with the operator on a phone is the acceptance path (not a code-only change).

## 3. Missing — GoodDollar with the wallet

GoodDollar still does not complete with the in-app wallet (the claim and, with it, the face
verification that gives the 7 profile points). Evidence and status (2026-10-01):

- **In-app wallet** (Chrome on a phone **and** desktop): the button answers
  `Claim failed: invalid params`. The browser console shows
  `GoodDollar claim error: Error: Invalid params` raised from the provider request.
  **Root cause found and fixed:** `lib/hooks/useWriteContract.ts` built `eth_sendTransaction`
  without `from`, and the in-app provider fills the nonce and estimates gas from `tx.from`
  (`fillTransaction` in `packages/pdj-wallet/src/provider.ts`); with `from` undefined the RPC
  answered "Invalid params" (-32602). The hook now sends `from` (the connected address), with a
  unit test (`lib/__tests__/useWriteContract.test.tsx`).
- **External wallet (OneKey)**: `execution reverted: UBIScheme: not whitelisted`. The address
  given, `0x2e2c4AC19c93d0984840cDD8E7f77500e2ef978e`, **is whitelisted and can claim**:
  verified on Celo mainnet (2026-10-01) with `getWhitelistedRoot(address)` → the address itself,
  `isWhitelisted` → true, `getWhitelistedOnChainId` → 42220, `checkEntitlement` →
  `115567513059601427369` (> 0), `UBIScheme.paused` → false, and a **`eth_call` simulation of
  `UBIScheme.claim()` from that address succeeds** (no revert). So with the R-#275 wiring the
  action for that address is `direct-claim`. The observed `not whitelisted` revert is therefore
  **unexplained by the current on-chain state**: likely a different account (`msg.sender`), a
  different chain or an attempt before the whitelisting. **Needs the OneKey transaction hash** to
  inspect `from`, the chain and the target contract.

The GoodDollar part is the problem R-#275 owns (the direct claim and the verification); R-#276
keeps the user-visible statement "GoodDollar does not work with the in-app wallet yet" until
R-#275 lands.

## 4. Transactions in the wallet panel — decided: on-chain

**Decision (operator, 2026-10-02):** the list of latest transactions the panel adds must be
**on-chain transactions**, not learn.tg's own `transaction` ledger.

### 4.1 Why on-chain, and not the app ledger

- The panel is a **wallet** surface: it must show what actually moved in that wallet, including
  activity done outside learn.tg (a transfer from an exchange, a swap). The `transaction` table only
  holds what learn.tg recorded.
- It needs **no new authenticated endpoint** and no DB query: it is the same public Blockscout read
  the collectibles tab already does (`explorerApiNftsUrl`).
- It keeps the panel **consistent with its own Send tab**: those sends go straight through the
  provider (`WalletPanel.handleSend`) and are never written to `transaction`, so the app ledger would
  omit the very transfers the panel just made.
- The `transaction` ledger keeps its own job (the business view: scholarship, donation, UBI,
  conversions) and the Transparency dashboard already reads it; it is not duplicated here.

### 4.2 Source (measured 2026-10-02)

- `GET {explorer}/api/v2/addresses/{address}/token-transfers` — **verified**: each item carries
  `from`/`to`, `token.symbol` and `token.decimals`, `total.value`, `timestamp`, `method`,
  `transaction_hash`, and `next_page_params` for pagination. Real output for the `.env` wallet:
  G$ claim (`115.567…`), SLEARN `payScholarship` (`93`), USDT transfers and CELO.
- `GET {explorer}/api/v2/addresses/{address}/transactions` — sibling v2 endpoint for the native and
  contract calls (outgoing transfers, fees); same shape.
- Reuse the existing helpers: `explorerAddressBase(network)` and `explorerTxUrl(hash, network)`
  (`lib/wallet-amounts.ts`). Tokens come with their own `decimals`, so `formatTokenAmount` already
  covers G$ (18), USDT (6), SLEARN (2) and CELO (18).

### 4.3 Observations and risks

- **Third-party dependency**, same as collectibles: latency, rate limits and schema changes. Degrade
  gracefully ("not available right now") and keep the link to the address on Blockscout; never leave a
  silent empty list.
- **Load lazily** when the tab opens, as the collectibles tab does (and as its test asserts: the NFTs
  are not requested when the panel opens). Do the same for transactions.
- **Mobile**: the `TabsList` is `grid-cols-4` (`WalletPanel.tsx:383`); a fifth tab is cramped on a
  360-px phone (R-#254 introduced the tabs for exactly this). Use a short label ("Activity") or a
  horizontally scrollable list.
- **Raw data**: show direction (in/out), token symbol, amount and date, with the explorer link; bound
  it to "latest N" (or paginate) instead of an unbounded list.
- Some rows repeat a transaction (one per token log, e.g. a `payScholarship` paying USDT and SLEARN);
  group by `transaction_hash` or accept the per-transfer rows deliberately.

### 4.4 Acceptance criteria (transactions)

- [ ] The panel lists the address's latest on-chain movements with token, direction (in/out), amount,
      date and a link to the explorer.
- [ ] Loaded lazily on tab open; a failure shows an honest message, never a silent empty list.
- [ ] The fifth tab does not break the phone layout (label or scrollable list), in English and Spanish.

- The GoodDollar claim is an on-chain transaction signed by the wallet: it inherits the gas rule
  (a little CELO for the network fee).
- The in-app wallet locks on reload and after an idle hour (R-#246): "locked" is a normal state,
  not an error.
- The GD course visibility by declared position is governed by R-#161/R-#192 and does not affect
  this requirement.
