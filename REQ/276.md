## 1. What works today (operator report, 2026-10-01, dev site)

Verified by hand and by the E2E `e2e/specs/wallet-panel-collectibles.spec.mjs`:

- create / import (recovery phrase or private key) / unlock (password, and the fingerprint/Face ID
  gesture), SIWE sign-in, disconnect and lock;
- the panel: the three balances (CELO/USDT/SLEARN), copy the address, receive with QR, send with
  destination/amount/balance validation and the fresh gesture before moving funds;
- **collectibles**: the panel's "Collectibles" tab lists the address's credentials read from
  Blockscout (4 shown for the `.env` wallet: Web3 & UBI, A relationship with Jesus, Donor,
  Connector). This is what the Web3 & UBI guide promises
  (`resources/en/web3-and-ubi/guide2.md`, "Step 4: Your collectibles").

So the in-app wallet is functional; the two items below remain.

## 2. To improve — visual design of the wallet panel

The operator finds the panel's visual appearance improvable. Starting points (not exhaustive):

- **The panel and the dialog are dense**: the tabs (balances/receive/send/collectibles), the copy
  button, the lock/close row and the notices compete for space on a 360-px phone; the panel opens
  from the header pill and should read as one clear surface.
- **Collectibles**: a 3-column grid of 64-px thumbnails with a truncated name; long course names
  (the same names the credentials truncate to 25 characters in their composed image, F-#195) get
  cut. Consider a larger, tappable item with the credential name and its date.
- **Dialog steps**: creation shows the password, the protection choice, the 12 words and the
  three-word confirmation in sequence; the wording and the emphasis of the primary action can be
  reviewed (the operator already reordered the protection step in R-#269).
- **Consistency**: the panel and the dialog are the only wallet UI in the app; align spacing, type
  scale and the use of the shadcn primitives across both.

A design pass with the operator on a phone is the acceptance path (not a code-only change).

## 3. Missing — GoodDollar with the wallet

GoodDollar still does not complete with the in-app wallet (the claim and, with it, the face
verification that gives the 7 profile points). Evidence and status (2026-10-01):

- **In-app wallet** (Chrome on a phone **and** desktop): the button answers
  `Claim failed: invalid params`. The browser console shows
  `GoodDollar claim error: Error: Invalid params` raised from the provider request.
  **Root cause found and fixed:** `lib/hooks/useWriteContract.ts` built `eth_sendTransaction`
  without `from`, and the in-app provider fills the nonce and estimates gas from `tx.from`
  (`fillTransaction` in `packages/pdj-wallet/src/provider.ts`); with `from` undefined the RPC
  answered "Invalid params" (-32602). The hook now sends `from` (the connected address), with a
  unit test (`lib/__tests__/useWriteContract.test.tsx`).
- **External wallet (OneKey)**: `execution reverted: UBIScheme: not whitelisted` for
  `0x2e2c4AC19c93d0984840cDD8E7f77500e2ef978e`. That address is **not whitelisted** in GoodDollar,
  so with the R-#275 wiring the button must resolve the action as `verify` (open GoodID) and
  **not** send `claim()`; a sent `claim()` that reverts means the deployed build did not have that
  wiring (or the whitelist read is wrong). Confirm the build and the read before closing R-#275.

The GoodDollar part is the problem R-#275 owns (the direct claim and the verification); R-#276
keeps the user-visible statement "GoodDollar does not work with the in-app wallet yet" until
R-#275 lands.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The wallet panel and dialog pass a design review with the operator on a phone (readable,
      tappable, consistent), in English and Spanish.
- [ ] The collectibles tab shows the credential name without truncating it to unreadability and
      the item is tappable (at least shows the full name and the date).
- [ ] An in-app wallet that is whitelisted in GoodDollar claims G$ (the `direct-claim` path of
      R-#275) and, when not whitelisted, the button opens the GoodID verification instead of
      sending a doomed `claim()`.
- [ ] The GoodDollar verification completes with the in-app wallet and sets
      `lastgooddollarverification` (7 profile points).
- [ ] `make type` and the affected suites green.

## 5. Notes

- The GoodDollar claim is an on-chain transaction signed by the wallet: it inherits the gas rule
  (a little CELO for the network fee).
- The in-app wallet locks on reload and after an idle hour (R-#246): "locked" is a normal state,
  not an error.
- The GD course visibility by declared position is governed by R-#161/R-#192 and does not affect
  this requirement.
