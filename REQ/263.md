## 1. Objective

Give a learner a **guided, interactive tool** to create family and business
budgets, based on the SBD course lessons 12+ and on real financial data from
**Diligent Records** (R-#258). The budget is compared against real execution
(budget vs. real) to help the learner make decisions.

The tool is the practice artifact of the course's budget lessons. It consumes
real income/expenses from **Diligent Records** and works with **Business Plan
Creator** (R-#262) for the plan section.

## 2. Data model (Kysely migration, `db/migrations/`)

### 2.1 `diligent_budget_template` — reusable budget templates

- `id` (PK)
- `usuario_id` (FK to `usuario`)
- `nombre` (varchar 255)
- `tipo` (`family` | `business` | `trip`)
- `lineas` (JSONB) — array of `{ account_code, monto_sugerido }`
- timestamps

### 2.2 `diligent_budget` — already in R-#258 (sibling)

The budget container (R-#258 §3.3) and its lines (R-#258 §3.4) are the same
entities. Budget Creator reuses them and adds a **template** layer for
onboarding.

### 2.3 `diligent_budget_export` — export history

- `id`, `usuario_id`, `budget_id`, `format` (`pdf` | `csv`), `created_at`

## 3. Design

1. **Offline first.** The tool reads its budget from IndexedDB and writes there
   first; a queue replays when the connection returns.

2. **Server routes** (all `authenticateUser` + `doc/api-security.md`):
   - `GET/POST /api/diligent/budget-templates`
   - `GET/POST /api/diligent/budgets`
   - `GET /api/diligent/budget-vs-real`
   - `GET /api/diligent/budget/pdf`

3. **Integration with Diligent Records (R-#258).** The `budget vs. real` view is
   computed from `GET /api/diligent/budget-vs-real` (already in R-#258). The tool
   does not duplicate data; it reads it.

4. **Integration with Business Plan Creator (R-#262).** The `budget` section of
   the business plan is computed from the budget of the linked `plan_id`. The
   tool does not duplicate data; it reads it.

5. **Templates.** The tool ships with default templates for:
   - Family budget (based on lesson 12: giving, saving, investing, water, food,
     housing, school, energy, communication, transport, medical, entertainment,
     clothing, other)
   - Business budget (based on lesson 11: merchandise, transport, wages,
     equipment, savings)
   - Trip budget (based on the operator's trip: lodging, food, transport,
     ministry, donations)

6. **PDF generation.** The budget can be exported as a PDF with:
   - Cover (name, period, currency)
   - Lines by account with planned amounts
   - Budget vs. real comparison
   - Notes

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An authenticated user can create a budget from a template and customize
      its lines.
- [ ] The `budget vs. real` view is computed from Diligent Records data (no
      duplication).
- [ ] The budget can be exported as a PDF with all lines and the comparison.
- [ ] The tool works offline; on reconnect it is replayed exactly once.
- [ ] `GET /api/diligent/budget*` and `POST /api/diligent/budget*` authenticate;
      `bin/audit-api-auth.mjs` reports 0 failed.
- [ ] Unit tests for the templates, the budget-vs-real calculation and the queue
      replay.
- [ ] `make type` and the full suite green.

## 5. Out of scope

- Formal certification or accreditation.
- Multi-user or shared budgets.
- Integration with SLEARN/SBT (that is in another session).
- The other tools (R-#258, R-#262)
