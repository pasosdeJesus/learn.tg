## 1. Objective

Give a learner a **guided, interactive tool** to create family and business
budgets, based on the SBD course lessons 12+ and on real financial data from
**Diligent Records light** (R-#258). The budget is compared against real
execution (budget vs. real) to help the learner make decisions.

The tool is the practice artifact of the course's budget lessons. It **consumes**
the R-#258 model for the budget container and lines, and for the budget-vs-real
comparison. It works alongside **Business Plan Creator** (R-#262), which also
consumes the same R-#258 model. The budget and the plan are **separate
documents**; the budget does not depend on the plan.

**No SLEARN, SBT or stable-sl in this tool.** Those belong to other REQ
(R-#192, R-#278, stable-sl) and are out of scope here.

## 2. What exists today

Nothing. The tool does not exist yet. The course of R-#257 is being written
and expects this tool for lesson 12+.

## 3. Data model

**This REQ does not duplicate the R-#258 domain model.** The tables
`diligent_plan`, `diligent_account`, `diligent_budget`, `diligent_budget_line`,
`diligent_transaction`, `diligent_transaction_entry`, `diligent_export` and
`diligent_upload` live in R-#258 §3. This REQ **adds one optional table** for
budget templates (onboarding) and **consumes** the R-#258 tables via API.

### 3.1 `diligent_budget_template` — reusable budget templates (this REQ's own table, optional)

- `id` (PK)
- `usuario_id` (FK to `usuario`)
- `nombre` (varchar 255)
- `tipo` (`family` | `business` | `trip`)
- `lineas` (JSONB) — array of `{ account_codigo, monto_sugerido, moneda }`
- timestamps

**This table is optional.** If the templates are simple constants in the code
(family, business, trip), the table can be omitted and the learner creates the
budget directly. The decision is left to the implementation; the REQ describes
the table in case reusable templates are wanted.

### 3.2 `diligent_budget` and `diligent_budget_line` — already in R-#258

The budget container (R-#258 §3.3) and its lines (R-#258 §3.4) are the same
entities. **Budget Creator edits them**; it does not define a second budget
table.

### 3.3 `diligent_budget_export` — export history (this REQ's own table)

- `id` (PK)
- `usuario_id` (FK to `usuario`)
- `budget_id` (FK to `diligent_budget` in R-#258)
- `format` (`pdf` | `json`)
- `created_at`

**R-#258's `diligent_export` is not reused**: it records exports of the
accounting domain (entries, plan of accounts), not of the budget document. Two
different concerns, two tables.

### 3.4 Derived, never stored

The budget vs. real comparison is **computed on read** from R-#258's
`GET /api/diligent/budget-vs-real`. It is not stored.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Offline first

The tool reads its budgets from IndexedDB and writes there first; a queue
replays when the connection returns, with `client_id` (UUID) for idempotency.
The tool reuses R-#258's offline plumbing (`lib/offline-db.ts`,
`lib/offline-queue-db.ts`, `useOfflineQueue`).

### 4.2 Server routes (all `authenticateUser` + `doc/api-security.md`)

This REQ adds **its own routes** for budget templates and budget exports. It
**does not add** new `diligent/plans`, `diligent/accounts`, `diligent/budgets`
or `diligent/entries` routes; those live in R-#258 and this tool **consumes**
them.

| Route | Methods | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `/api/diligent/budget-templates` | GET, POST | List/create templates (optional) |
| `/api/diligent/budget-templates/[id]` | GET, PATCH, DELETE | Template detail/edit |
| `/api/diligent/budgets` | GET, POST | **Consumed from R-#258**; this REQ edits the lines |
| `/api/diligent/budgets/[id]/pdf` | GET | Generate the budget PDF |
| `/api/diligent/budgets/[id]/export` | POST | Record an export in `diligent_budget_export` |

The budget container and its lines are created/edited through **R-#258's**
`/api/diligent/budgets` and `/api/diligent/budgets/[id]` routes (POST/PATCH with
`lines`). This REQ does not duplicate those routes.

### 4.3 Integration with Diligent Records (R-#258) — consume, do not duplicate

The **budget vs. real** view is computed from **R-#258's**
`GET /api/diligent/budget-vs-real` (per account and period). The tool does not
duplicate data; it reads it.

The budget container and lines are edited through R-#258's routes. The tool
adds the **editing UI** (templates, line editor, PDF), not a second model.

The API contract is **versioned** (`{ version: 1, data: ... }`, R-#258 §4.10).
This tool must handle `version: 1` and treat an empty response as "no data yet"
(empty array, not error).

### 4.4 Integration with Business Plan Creator (R-#262) — sibling, not dependency

Business Plan Creator is a **sibling tool**, not a dependency. Both consume
R-#258. The plan's `budget` section is computed from R-#258 directly (via
`/api/diligent/budget-vs-real`), not from this tool. If this tool adds a
template or a view, that is this REQ's concern; the plan does not depend on it.

**No circular dependency:** R-#263 does not import R-#262's code, tables or
routes. Both depend on R-#258.

### 4.5 Templates

The tool ships with default templates as **constants in the code** (or in
`diligent_budget_template` if reusable templates are wanted):

- **Family budget** (based on lesson 12: giving, saving, investing, water, food,
  housing, school, energy, communication, transport, medical, entertainment,
  clothing, other)
- **Business budget** (based on lesson 11: merchandise, transport, wages,
  equipment, savings)
- **Trip budget** (lodging, food, transport, ministry, donations)

The learner can apply a template to a new budget, then customize the lines. The
template is a starting point, not a constraint.

### 4.6 PDF generation

The budget can be exported as a PDF with:

- Cover (name, period, currency)
- Lines by account with planned amounts
- Budget vs. real comparison (computed on read from R-#258)
- Notes

The PDF is generated **server-side** and recorded in `diligent_budget_export`.

### 4.7 Privacy

The budget is private to the user. No third-party names leave the server. Notes
are free text but the UI discourages personal names. The route list is audited
with `bin/audit-api-auth.mjs`.

### 4.8 Simple mode

The tool shows the budget lines and the budget-vs-real comparison. The learner
can hide the comparison until they have real data (empty comparison shows a
notice: "Once you record entries in Diligent Records, the comparison will
appear here"). There is no separate "simple mode" toggle; the data availability
drives the view.

### 4.9 Money in the learner's terms

Amounts are entered as text with two decimals and stored as `numeric(14,2)`
(R-#258 model); the currency is per account (Leones and USD side by side, no
conversion). The tool does not convert or move money.

### 4.10 No SLEARN, SBT or stable-sl

The tool does not touch SLEARN, SBTs or stable-sl. Those are out of scope
(R-#192, R-#278, stable-sl).

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An authenticated user can create a budget (with or without a template) and
      customize its lines.
- [ ] The budget container and lines are stored in **R-#258's**
      `diligent_budget` and `diligent_budget_line` (no second budget table).
- [ ] The **budget vs. real** view is computed **on read** from R-#258's
      `/api/diligent/budget-vs-real` (no duplication, no stored comparison).
- [ ] The budget can be exported as a PDF with all lines and the computed
      comparison.
- [ ] The tool works offline; on reconnect it is replayed exactly once
      (`client_id`).
- [ ] The tool handles the "no Diligent Records data yet" case (empty response,
      not error) and shows the notice.
- [ ] `GET /api/diligent/budget*` and `POST /api/diligent/budget*`
      authenticate; `bin/audit-api-auth.mjs` reports **0 failed**.
- [ ] Unit tests for the templates, the budget-vs-real calculation (with mocked
      R-#258 responses), the PDF generation and the queue replay.
- [ ] `make type` and the full suite green.

## 6. Out of scope

- Formal certification or accreditation.
- Multi-user or shared budgets.
- **SLEARN, SBT and stable-sl integration** (out of scope for this REQ; see
  R-#192, R-#278, stable-sl).
- The other tools (R-#258, R-#262) themselves: this REQ consumes R-#258's model
  and does not depend on R-#262.
- The detailed mapping between each course lesson and each budget template
  (that is R-#257's concern).
- The full edition of Diligent Records (separate application, another REQ).
